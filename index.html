A Smart Shopping Day at the Market

Date: 2026-10-18 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Markets and shopping

先读英文，再对照中文，注意购物常用表达。

On Saturday morning, the local market is busy but friendly. Shoppers walk slowly between small stalls and compare prices before they buy. Some people bring their own bags because they want to reduce waste. Others simply enjoy the fresh air, the bright colors, and the warm voices of sellers.

周六早上，当地市场忙碌又友好。购物者在摊位间慢慢走，买前比较价格。有些人自带袋子以减少浪费，另一些人则享受新鲜空气、鲜艳色彩和卖家的热情声音。

A good shopper does not always choose the cheapest thing. It is wiser to check quality, size, and freshness. For example, fruit should look and smell pleasant, and clothes should feel comfortable. If a price seems too high, it is fine to ask politely for a small discount or to wait for a better offer.

聪明的购物者不总选最便宜的东西。更明智的是检查质量、尺寸和新鲜度。例如，水果应看起来和闻起来不错，衣服应穿着舒适。如果价格太高，可以礼貌地要求小折扣，或等待更好的优惠。

At the end of the trip, many people feel happy with their simple purchases. Shopping well is not about spending more; it is about making careful choices. A calm mind, a clear list, and a friendly smile can turn an ordinary market visit into a pleasant and useful experience.

购物结束时，许多人对自己简单的购买感到满意。会购物不是花更多钱，而是做谨慎选择。平静的心态、清晰的清单和友好的微笑，能把普通的市场之行变成愉快有用的经历。
