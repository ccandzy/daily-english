Learn New Words with Small Daily Steps

Date: 2026-10-19 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Language Learning Tips

每天学五个新词，并放进真实句子里，坚持复习。

Learning new words can feel difficult, but small daily steps really help. Try to learn five new words each day. Write them in a small notebook and say them aloud. This simple habit makes your memory stronger and your pronunciation clearer. Over time, you will know many useful words.

学习新单词可能感觉很难，但每天的小步骤真的很有帮助。试着每天学五个新单词。把它们写在一个小笔记本里，并大声说出来。这个简单的习惯会让你的记忆更强，发音更清楚。久而久之，你会认识许多有用的单词。

Use the words in real sentences. For example, if you learn the word &quot;delicious,&quot; describe your lunch to a friend. When you connect a word to your own life, you remember it longer. Also, review the words after one day and again after one week. Quick reviews are more effective than one long study session.

在真实句子中使用这些单词。例如，如果你学了“delicious”这个词，就向朋友描述你的午餐。当你把单词和自己的生话联系起来时，你会记得更久。另外，一天后复习这些单词，一周后再复习一次。快速复习比一次长时间学习更有效。

Do not worry about mistakes. Every mistake is a chance to learn something new. Speak with friends or record your voice on your phone. Listen to the recording and notice what you can improve. Keep going, and you will get better step by step. Remember that progress takes time, so be patient with yourself.

不要担心犯错。每个错误都是学习新东西的机会。和朋友交谈，或者用手机录下自己的声音。听录音，注意哪些地方可以改进。坚持下去，你会一步一步变得更好。记住进步需要时间，所以对自己有耐心。
