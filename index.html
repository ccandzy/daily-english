Designing for Everyone: The Curb Cut Effect

Date: 2026-11-15 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: Technology accessibility and inclusive design — how features made for disabled users end up helping everyone

阅读时注意“为少数人设计，却让所有人受益”这个核心观点，并留意文中的因果关系词（so, because, this means）。

When you walk down a street, you may see a small ramp at the corner. It cuts the curb so that a person in a wheelchair can cross the road safely. This simple idea is called a curb cut. It was first built for people with disabilities. But today, almost everyone uses it. Parents with baby strollers use it. People pulling heavy suitcases use it. Delivery workers with hand trucks use it too. So a design made for a small group can help a much larger group.

当你走在街上时，你可能会看到街角有一个小坡道。它把路缘切开，让坐轮椅的人能够安全过马路。这个简单的想法叫做“路缘坡”。它最初是为残障人士建造的。但今天，几乎每个人都在用它。推婴儿车的父母用它。拉着沉重行李箱的人用它。推着手推车的送货员也用它。所以，为一个小群体做的设计，也能帮助一个大得多的群体。

This pattern appears in technology as well. Think about voice typing on a phone. It was made to help people who cannot use their hands easily. Now many people use it while driving or cooking, because their hands are busy. Another example is text-to-speech. It reads words on a screen out loud. Blind users need it to use a computer. But busy people also listen to articles this way on the bus. This means that accessible features often become popular features.

这种模式在科技中也同样出现。想想手机上的语音输入。它原本是为了帮助那些手不方便使用的人。现在很多人在开车或做饭时用它，因为他们的手很忙。另一个例子是文字转语音。它把屏幕上的文字大声读出来。盲人用户需要它才能使用电脑。但忙碌的人也会在公交车上用这种方式听文章。这意味着，无障碍功能常常会变成受欢迎的功能。

So good designers do not treat accessibility as extra work. They see it as a way to make products better for everyone. When you design for the person who faces the most difficulty, you often find a simple solution that helps all users. This is a useful lesson. Next time you use a helpful feature, ask yourself: who was this really made for?

所以优秀的设计师不会把无障碍当作额外的工作。他们把它看作让产品对每个人都更好的方法。当你为面对最大困难的人设计时，你常常会找到一个帮助所有用户的简单方案。这是一个有用的道理。下次你使用一个有用的功能时，问问自己：这到底是为谁做的？

Grammar

语法小课堂

被动语态（一般过去时）

文中原句：It was first built for people with disabilities.

结构：主语 + was/were + 过去分词 (+ by …)

说明：强调动作的承受者而不是执行者，常用于说明某物“被建造、被制造、被使用”的情况。

仿写：This bridge was built in 1990.

这座桥建于1990年。

目的状语从句（so that）

文中原句：It cuts the curb so that a person in a wheelchair can cross the road safely.

结构：主句 + so that + 主语 + can/could + 动词原形

说明：用 so that 引出目的，表示“为了……”，后面常跟 can、could 等情态动词。

仿写：She spoke slowly so that everyone could understand her.

她说得很慢，以便每个人都能听懂她。

定语从句（who 引导）

文中原句：When you design for the person who faces the most difficulty, you often find a simple solution that helps all users.

结构：名词 + who + 动词 …（修饰人）

说明：who 引导定语从句，用来修饰前面的人，说明“什么样的人”。注意从句紧跟被修饰的名词。

仿写：I know a teacher who speaks three languages.

我认识一位会说三种语言的老师。
