Birdwatching in the City

Date: 2026-10-13 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Nature and outdoors — a simple hobby in city parks

先读一遍英文，再对照中文，注意动词和介词的搭配。

Many people in big cities are now trying a simple hobby: birdwatching. You do not need expensive tools. A small notebook, comfortable shoes, and patience are enough. City parks and riverside paths are often full of birds if you look carefully. The idea is simple: watch, listen, and enjoy.

现在，许多大城市里的人都在尝试一个简单的爱好：观鸟。你不需要昂贵的工具。一个小本子、一双舒服的鞋和耐心就够了。如果你仔细观察，城市公园和河边小路常常有很多鸟。方法很简单：看、听、享受。

The best time is early morning. Birds are busy finding food then, and the streets are quiet. Walk slowly, keep your voice low, and stop often. If you stand still for a few minutes, birds may come closer. This teaches you to be calm and to notice small things.

最好的时间是清晨。那时鸟儿正忙着找食物，街道也很安静。慢慢走，压低声音，经常停下来。如果你静止站几分钟,鸟儿可能会靠近一些。这教会你平静下来,并注意到细小的事物。

Birdwatching is also good for your health. It takes you outside, away from screens, and gives your eyes a rest. It costs almost nothing, and you can do it alone or with a friend. Many beginners say they feel happier after an hour in nature. You can start this weekend — just find a green place near your home and look up.

观鸟对你的健康也有好处。它带你到户外，远离屏幕，让你的眼睛休息一下。它几乎不花钱，你可以自己做，也可以和朋友一起做。很多初学者说，在大自然里待一小时后他们会更开心。这个周末你就可以开始——只要在你家附近找一块绿地，然后抬头看看。
