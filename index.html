Can Animals Warn Each Other About Danger?

Date: 2026-11-09 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: Animal intelligence and communication — alarm calls and the trade-off between warning others and staying hidden

阅读时先找出“动物怎样发出警告”和“发出警告有什么风险”这两个重点，再注意文中具体的动物例子。

Many animals live in groups, and some of them use special sounds to warn each other about danger. When a bird sees a cat or a hawk, it may make a short, loud call. Other birds hear the sound and quickly hide in trees or fly away. This simple system can save many lives. However, the animal that makes the call also tells the enemy where it is. So the caller takes a real risk.

很多动物成群生活，其中一些会用特殊的声音互相警告危险。当一只鸟看到猫或鹰时，它可能会发出短促响亮的叫声。其他鸟听到声音，就赶快躲进树里或飞走。这个简单的系统能救很多生命。不过，发出叫声的动物也会让敌人知道它在哪里。所以，报警者承担着真实的风险。

Scientists have studied this problem in meerkats, small animals that live in dry parts of Africa. One meerkat often stands tall and watches for danger while others look for food. If it sees a snake or an eagle, it gives a different call for each enemy. The group then knows what to do: stand together or run to a hole. The watcher is not always the same animal. They take turns, so no single meerkat is always in danger.

科学家在猫鼬身上研究过这个问题。猫鼬是生活在非洲干旱地区的小动物。通常有一只猫鼬站得高高的，在别的猫鼬找食物时观察危险。如果它看到蛇或鹰，就会为不同的敌人发出不同的叫声。群体于是知道该怎么做：聚在一起，或者跑进洞里。观察者不总是同一只动物。它们轮流来做，所以没有哪只猫鼬总是处在危险中。

This shows a simple trade-off in animal communication. A clear warning helps the group, but it can hurt the caller. Taking turns is one clever answer. Another answer is a quiet signal, like a small movement of the tail, that only close friends can see. So animal “language” is not only about sounds. It is also about choosing when to speak and when to stay silent.

这显示了动物交流中的一个简单取舍。清楚的警告对群体有帮助，但可能伤害发出警告的动物。轮流来做是一个聪明的办法。另一个办法是安静的信号，比如尾巴轻轻一动，只有身边的朋友能看到。所以动物的“语言”不只是声音。它也关乎选择什么时候出声、什么时候保持安静。
