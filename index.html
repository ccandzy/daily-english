水不够时，城市如何选择用水顺序

Date: 2026-11-23 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: water scarcity and resource management — how cities decide which water use comes first

阅读时先找出“谁先用水、谁后用水”的顺序，再注意作者用什么简单例子说明取舍。

When a city does not have enough water, it cannot give the same amount to everyone at the same time. Many cities use a simple idea: put water uses in order. Drinking water and hospitals come first. Homes and schools come next. Then come shops, farms, and factories. This order is not perfect, but it helps people make hard choices quickly. For example, during a dry summer, a city may stop washing streets and filling public pools. It may also ask people to take shorter showers. These small actions save water for the most important needs.

当一个城市没有足够的水时，它不能在同一时间给每个人同样的水量。许多城市用一个简单的想法：把用水顺序排好。饮用水和医院排在第一位。家庭和学校排在后面。然后是商店、农场和工厂。这个顺序并不完美，但它帮助人们快速做出艰难的选择。例如，在干旱的夏天，城市可能停止冲洗街道和给公共泳池注水。它也可能请人们缩短洗澡时间。这些小小的行动为最重要的需求节省了水。

The hard part is the middle of the list. A farm needs water to grow food. A factory needs water to make things and to keep workers safe. A family needs water to cook and clean. Which one should wait? There is no easy answer. One useful step is to ask what happens if we cut water here. If a factory stops for a week, some people may lose pay. If a farm loses water, food prices may rise later. Cities often talk with these groups before they make a plan. They also try to fix leaks first, because lost water helps no one.

困难的部分是名单的中间。农场需要水来种粮食。工厂需要水来制造东西并保证工人安全。家庭需要水来做饭和清洁。哪一个应该等？没有简单的答案。一个有用的步骤是问：如果我们在这里减少水，会发生什么？如果工厂停工一周，有些人可能会少拿工资。如果农场缺水，以后食品价格可能上涨。城市在制定计划前，常常和这些群体沟通。他们也先努力修理漏水，因为漏掉的水对谁都没有帮助。

A good water plan also looks at time. Some choices are for today, and some are for next year. Today, a city may limit garden watering. Next year, it may build a system to catch rainwater or clean used water for parks. This is a trade-off: big systems cost money now, but they help later. Small habits are cheaper and start at once. The best plans use both. They ask people to save a little every day, and they also prepare for the next dry year. In this way, a city can share a limited resource more fairly.

一个好的用水计划也会考虑时间。有些选择是为了今天，有些是为了明年。今天，城市可能限制花园浇水。明年，它可能建造收集雨水或净化废水用于公园的系统。这是一种取舍：大系统现在花钱，但以后有帮助。小习惯更便宜，而且马上就能开始。最好的计划两者都用。它们请人们每天节省一点，也为下一个干旱年份做准备。这样，城市可以更公平地分享有限的资源。

Grammar

语法小课堂

一般现在时表示普遍情况

文中原句：Many cities use a simple idea: put water uses in order.

结构：Subject + verb (simple present) + object

说明：一般现在时用来描述经常发生的事、普遍做法或事实。这里说明许多城市通常采用的做法。

仿写：Many families save water at home.

许多家庭在家里节约用水。

情态动词 may 表示可能性

文中原句：For example, during a dry summer, a city may stop washing streets and filling public pools.

结构：Subject + may + verb

说明：may 表示“可能”，语气比 will 更不确定，常用于说明可能发生的情况或措施。

仿写：A city may ask people to take shorter showers.

城市可能会请人们缩短洗澡时间。

第一条件句（if + 一般现在时，主句用 may）

文中原句：If a factory stops for a week, some people may lose pay.

结构：If + subject + simple present, subject + may + verb

说明：第一条件句用来谈论真实可能发生的情况。if 从句用一般现在时，主句常用 may、will 等表示结果。

仿写：If a farm loses water, food prices may rise.

如果农场缺水，食品价格可能会上涨。
