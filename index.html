Why Sleeping In on Weekends Can Leave You Tired on Monday

Date: 2026-10-25 | Level: B1–B2 (target) | Topic: Sleep science and daily energy — weekend sleep timing and Monday energy

阅读时注意“睡眠时间点”如何影响白天精力，并留意因果与转折表达。

Imagine you wake at seven on weekdays but sleep until ten on Saturday and Sunday. By Monday morning, you may feel groggy even after two long lie-ins. This happens because your body clock, or circadian rhythm, expects a regular pattern. When weekend wake-up times move later, the clock shifts a little, like a slow clock that needs resetting. You may then struggle to fall asleep on Sunday night and feel low on energy on Monday. The extra rest can feel pleasant, but it may not leave you as refreshed as you expect.

想象你工作日七点起床，但周六周日睡到十点。到了周一早上，即使两次长时间睡懒觉，你可能仍感到昏昏沉沉。这是因为你的身体时钟，也就是昼夜节律，期待规律的模式。当周末起床时间后移，时钟会稍微偏移，就像一只需要重设的慢钟。你可能因此周日晚难以入睡，周一精力不足。额外的休息可能很舒服，但未必让你像预期那样神清气爽。

The key is not only how many hours you sleep but when you sleep. Your circadian rhythm is shaped by light and by consistent wake-up times. Morning light tells your brain that the day has started, while darkness in the evening helps prepare you for sleep. If you sleep in and stay in a dark room, your brain receives weaker signals, so your internal clock may drift. The result is a kind of mild jet lag without a flight. Your sleep pressure also builds during the day, and a late lie-in can reduce it at bedtime.

关键不仅在于你睡多少小时，还在于你什么时候睡。你的昼夜节律受光线和固定起床时间影响。晨光告诉大脑一天已经开始，而夜晚的黑暗帮助身体准备入睡。如果你睡懒觉并待在暗房间里，大脑收到的信号较弱，内部时钟可能逐渐偏移。结果就像没有坐飞机的轻微时差。你的睡眠压力也在白天积累，而太晚起床会降低睡前的睡眠压力。

Does this mean you should never catch up on sleep? Not exactly. If you are truly short of sleep, a longer rest can help, especially after a busy week. The trade-off is that a big change in timing may make Monday harder. A gentler approach is to keep your wake-up time within about an hour of your usual time, even at weekends, and to get some morning light soon after waking. On a free day, a short nap early in the afternoon may restore energy without confusing your body clock too much.

这是否意味着你永远不该补觉？不完全是。如果你确实睡眠不足，更长的休息会有帮助，尤其是在忙碌一周之后。代价是，作息时间大幅改变可能让周一更难熬。更温和的做法是，即使周末也把起床时间控制在与平常相差约一小时以内，并在醒来后不久接触晨光。在空闲日，下午早些时候短暂小睡可以恢复精力，又不会太扰乱生物钟。
