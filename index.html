Reading in Everyday Life

Date: 2026-10-15 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Books and Reading Habits

先通读一遍了解大意，再逐句对照中文；注意文中"prefer / suggest / recommend"这类表示喜好和建议的动词用法。

Many people today read books on their phones or tablets. It is easy and cheap, and you can carry hundreds of stories in your pocket. However, some readers still prefer paper books. They enjoy the smell of the pages and the feeling of turning them. Studies suggest both ways can help you learn new words and relax after a busy day.

今天很多人用手机或平板电脑看书。这既方便又便宜，你还能把几百个故事装进口袋里。不过，有些读者仍然更喜欢纸质书。他们喜欢书页的气味，也喜欢翻页的感觉。研究显示，两种方式都能帮你学新单词，并在忙碌一天后放松下来。

If you want to read more, start small. Choose a book that matches your level, not one that looks impressive. Try to read ten pages every evening before you sleep. When you meet a difficult word, do not stop too long. Guess its meaning first, then check it later. In this way, reading becomes a habit, not a chore.

如果你想读得更多，就从小处开始。选一本适合你水平的书，而不是看起来很高深的书。试着每晚睡前读十页。遇到难词时，不要停太久。先猜意思，之后再查。这样，阅读就会变成习惯，而不是苦差事。

Sharing also makes reading more fun. You can join a library club or a small group online. Members often recommend books and discuss the characters. Sometimes a friend&#39;s suggestion is better than any advertisement. So pick a story this week, and tell someone about it.

分享也会让阅读更有趣。你可以加入图书馆的读书会，或一个线上小组。成员常常推荐书籍，并讨论书中的人物。有时候，朋友的推荐比任何广告都更好。所以这周挑一个故事，然后讲给别人听吧。
