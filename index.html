Solar Power at Night: The Battery Question

Date: 2026-11-18 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: Renewable energy and electricity storage — storing solar power in home batteries

阅读时先抓住“问题—办法—取舍”这条线，再回头记短语，理解会更牢。

Solar panels make electricity only when the sun is bright. But most homes use the most power in the evening. At that time, the sun is down, so the panels produce nothing. This is a simple problem, but it is important. A home can send extra power to the grid during the day. Then, in the evening, it can buy power back. This works, but the price is not always the same. Power can be cheap at noon and expensive at night.

太阳能板只有在阳光充足时才能发电。但大多数家庭在傍晚用电最多。那时太阳已经下山，所以太阳能板什么也发不出来。这是一个简单的问题，但很重要。一个家庭可以在白天把多余的电送到电网。然后到了傍晚，再买回电来用。这行得通，但价格并不总是一样的。中午的电可能很便宜，晚上的电却很贵。

A home battery is another answer. It stores the extra power during the day. In the evening, the family uses this stored power instead of buying it. For example, a family may cook dinner and watch TV after work. With a battery, they can use their own solar power at that time. The battery does not make new power. It just moves power from one time to another. This is useful because the sun and daily life do not always match.

家用电池是另一种解决办法。它在白天把多余的电储存起来。到了傍晚，这家人就用这些存起来的电，而不是去买电。例如，一家人可能在下班后做晚饭、看电视。有了电池，他们就能在那时用上自己家的太阳能电。电池并不产生新的电。它只是把电从一个时间移到另一个时间。这很有用，因为太阳和日常生活并不总是对得上。

But a battery has a cost. It is not cheap, and it takes up space in the home. It also holds only a limited amount of power. On a cloudy day, it may not fill up. So a family must decide: is the battery worth the money? For some homes, yes. They save money over time and feel safer when the grid has problems. For others, the cost is too high. The best choice depends on the home, the weather, and the local power prices.

但电池是有代价的。它不便宜，而且在家里要占地方。它储存的电量也有限。阴天的时候，它可能充不满。所以一个家庭必须决定：这块电池值这些钱吗？对一些家庭来说，值得。他们长期能省钱，而且电网出问题时也更有安全感。对另一些家庭来说，成本太高了。最好的选择取决于这个家庭、天气和当地的电价。

一般现在时表示普遍事实

文中原句：Solar panels make electricity only when the sun is bright.

结构：主语 + 动词原形/动词-s + 其他

说明：一般现在时用来描述经常发生或普遍成立的事情。这里说明太阳能板发电的一般规律。

仿写：Plants need water and light to grow.

植物需要水和光才能生长。

instead of + 动名词

文中原句：In the evening, the family uses this stored power instead of buying it.

结构：主语 + 动词 + 宾语 + instead of + doing

说明：instead of 表示“而不是”，后面接名词或动名词（-ing），用来说明替代的做法。

仿写：She walked to work instead of taking the bus.

她走路去上班，而不是坐公交车。

情态动词 must 表示“必须”

文中原句：So a family must decide: is the battery worth the money?

结构：主语 + must + 动词原形

说明：must 表示必要或必须做的事，后面直接跟动词原形，没有人称变化。

仿写：You must show your ticket at the door.

你必须在门口出示你的票。
