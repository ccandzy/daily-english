Free Apps and Your Personal Data

Date: 2026-11-05 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: Digital privacy and personal data — the trade-off between free apps and personal data

阅读时先找出“免费应用用什么换取你的数据”，再注意文中表示因果和对比的连接词（so, but, instead）。

Many apps on your phone are free. You do not pay money to use them. So how do these companies make money? Often, they collect your personal data. This includes your location, your search history, and the things you click on. They use this data to show you ads. Some companies also sell the data to other businesses. For users, the app feels free, but the real price is information about their daily life.

你手机上的很多应用都是免费的。你使用它们不用付钱。那么这些公司怎么赚钱呢？它们常常收集你的个人数据。这包括你的位置、搜索记录，以及你点击的东西。它们用这些数据给你看广告。有些公司还会把数据卖给其他企业。对用户来说，这个应用感觉是免费的，但真正的代价是关于他们日常生活的信息。

There is a simple way to think about this. Imagine a shop that gives you a free cup of tea every morning. In return, the shop owner writes down what time you come, what you wear, and who you talk to. Most people would feel uncomfortable. Free apps work in a similar way. You get a useful service, and the company gets a picture of your habits. The trade-off is clear: you save money, but you give up some privacy.

有一个简单的方法来想这件事。想象一家商店每天早上给你一杯免费的茶。作为回报，店主会记下你几点来、你穿什么、你和谁说话。大多数人会觉得不舒服。免费应用也是类似的方式。你得到一个有用的服务，公司得到一幅关于你习惯的画像。这个取舍很清楚：你省了钱，但你放弃了一些隐私。

You do not need to delete every app. Instead, take small steps. First, check the privacy settings on your phone and turn off location sharing for apps that do not need it. Second, ask yourself a simple question before you download something: "What does this app really need to know about me?" Third, use the app less if the ads feel too personal. Small choices like these help you keep more control over your data.

你不需要删掉每一个应用。相反，可以采取一些小步骤。第一，检查手机的隐私设置，关掉那些不需要位置的应用的位置共享。第二，在下载某个东西之前问自己一个简单的问题：“这个应用真的需要了解我什么？”第三，如果广告感觉太针对个人，就少用这个应用。像这样的小选择能帮你更好地掌控自己的数据。
