Why Salt Makes Fermented Cabbage Sour and Crunchy

Date: 2026-10-31 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: fermentation and food culture — salt in cabbage fermentation

学习重点：理解盐在发酵中的双重作用，并注意 however 引出的转折。

Imagine a jar of cabbage on a kitchen table. After a few days, it smells sour and tastes sharp. This is fermentation. Tiny living things called bacteria change the sugar in the cabbage. In food culture, people use this old method to keep vegetables for winter. The question is simple: why do we add salt first? It sounds strange because salt does not feed bacteria.

想象厨房桌上有一罐卷心菜。几天后，它闻起来酸，吃起来味道很冲。这就是发酵。叫做细菌的微小生物会改变卷心菜里的糖。在饮食文化中，人们用这种古老方法把蔬菜存到冬天。问题很简单：为什么我们要先加盐？这听起来很奇怪，因为盐并不喂养细菌。

Salt does two useful jobs. First, it pulls water out of the cabbage and makes a salty liquid called brine. The brine covers the cabbage and keeps air away. Second, salt slows many bad bacteria. Good bacteria can still work, but more slowly. This gives the good bacteria time to make acid. The acid makes the food sour and safe. However, too much salt can stop everything and make the cabbage very salty.

盐有两个有用的作用。第一，它把水从卷心菜里拉出来，形成一种叫盐水的咸液体。盐水盖住卷心菜，并隔绝空气。第二，盐会减慢许多坏细菌。好细菌仍能工作，但会更慢。这给好细菌时间产生酸。酸让食物变酸并变得安全。然而，盐太多会阻止一切，还会让卷心菜非常咸。

For home cooks, the lesson is to weigh the salt and keep the cabbage under the brine. You do not need a special machine. A clean jar and a little patience are enough. Taste after a few days, then move the jar to the fridge. The salt is not just for flavor. It helps decide which bacteria grow and how crunchy the cabbage stays. That is a small balance between safety and speed.

对家庭厨师来说，要点是称好盐，并让卷心菜保持在盐水下面。你不需要特别的机器。一个干净的罐子和一点耐心就够了。几天后尝一尝，然后把罐子放进冰箱。盐不只是为了味道。它帮助决定哪些细菌生长，以及卷心菜能保持多脆。这是安全与速度之间的小平衡。
