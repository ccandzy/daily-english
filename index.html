Padel: A New Sport for Everyone

Date: 2026-10-17 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Sports — Padel (a popular new racket sport)

先通读三遍，注意“easy to learn”“play together”这类短语搭配；读完后试着用英文说出你最喜欢的一项运动。

Padel is a sport that mixes tennis and squash. It is played on a small court with glass walls. Players use short rackets and a soft ball. Many people enjoy padel because it is easy to learn, and it is fun from the very first game. You do not need to be very strong to play well.

帕德尔是一项结合了网球和壁球特点的运动。它在带玻璃墙的小型球场上进行。球员使用短球拍和软球。很多人喜欢帕德尔，因为它容易学会，而且从第一场球开始就很有趣。你不需要很强壮也能打得好。

The court is smaller than a tennis court, so players do not run very far. Two teams of two players hit the ball against the walls, and this makes the game fast and exciting. The ball can touch the wall before you hit it back. You can play padel outside or inside, in winter or summer.

球场比网球场小，所以球员不用跑很远。两队各两名球员把球打向墙壁，这让比赛又快又刺激。球可以先碰到墙，然后你再把它打回去。帕德尔既可以在室外打，也可以在室内打，冬天夏天都可以。

In recent years, padel has become popular in many countries around the world. Young people and older people often play together, so it is a friendly and social sport. If you want to try something new this year, padel may be a great choice. It keeps you healthy without making you too tired.

近年来，帕德尔在世界许多国家变得很受欢迎。年轻人和年纪大的人常常一起打球，所以它是一项友好、有社交性的运动。如果你今年想尝试新东西，帕德尔可能是个很好的选择。它让你保持健康，又不会让你太累。
