Green Bridges: Helping Animals Cross Busy Roads

Date: 2026-10-30 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: biodiversity and wildlife conservation — wildlife corridors and road crossings

阅读时先找“问题—办法—行动”，并注意 habitat、corridor 等词在上下文中的意思。

Imagine a deer standing beside a busy road. It needs to reach the forest on the other side, but cars pass quickly. Many animals face this problem when roads cut through their habitat. A habitat is the natural home of a plant or animal. When a road splits a forest into small pieces, animals cannot easily find food and water, or meet other animals. This is called habitat fragmentation.

想象一只鹿站在繁忙的公路旁。它需要到达另一边的森林，但汽车飞快地驶过。当道路穿过动物的栖息地时，许多动物都会面临这个问题。栖息地是植物或动物的自然家园。当一条路把森林分成小块时，动物就不容易找到食物和水，也不容易遇到其他动物。这被称为栖息地破碎化。

One simple solution is a wildlife corridor. This is a safe path that connects two natural areas. It can be a line of trees, a tunnel under the road, or a wide bridge over it. A green bridge is covered with grass and plants, so animals feel safe. Such paths help animals move without meeting cars. However, they cost money to build and need care.

一个简单的解决办法是野生动物走廊。这是一条连接两个自然区域的安全通道。它可以是成排的树木、路下的隧道，或路上方的一座宽桥。绿色桥梁上覆盖着草和植物，所以动物会感到安全。这样的通道帮助动物移动，而不会遇到汽车。然而，建造它们要花钱，也需要维护。

People can help by supporting local nature projects. They can also ask road planners to include safe crossings for animals. After a bridge is built, experts monitor it. They check which animals use it and when. This shows if the bridge works. If it does not, small changes can help. Safe crossings are a practical way to protect biodiversity, the variety of life around us.

人们可以通过支持当地的自然项目来帮忙。他们也可以要求道路规划者为动物设置安全通道。桥梁建成后，专家会监测它。他们会检查哪些动物使用它，以及什么时候使用。这能说明桥是否有效。如果无效，做一些小改动也会有帮助。安全通道是保护生物多样性——即我们周围生命的多样性——的一种实用方式。
