Esports: Sports in the Digital Age

Date: 2026-09-07 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Esports

先整体读一遍英文，再看中文翻译，注意动词和名词的搭配。

Esports are video game competitions watched by millions of fans. Players train like traditional athletes, practicing many hours every day. They use quick hands, smart strategies, and calm minds under pressure. Many people now see esports as a real sport because it needs teamwork and strong focus. However, some people still think playing games is only entertainment.

电子竞技是被数百万人观看的视频游戏比赛。选手们像传统运动员一样训练，每天练习很多小时。他们运用敏捷的双手、聪明的策略以及在压力下冷静的心态。许多人现在把电子竞技看作真正的运动，因为它需要团队合作和高度专注。然而，一些人仍然认为打游戏只是娱乐。

Professional esports players often compete in large arenas in front of excited crowds. Their matches are broadcast online, so viewers from different countries can watch at the same time. Teams have coaches, analysts, and even fitness trainers. A healthy body and a clear mind help players perform better. As technology improves, esports may become more popular than many traditional sports.

职业电子竞技选手经常在大型竞技场里，面对热情的观众比赛。他们的比赛在网上播出，所以来自不同国家的观众可以同时观看。队伍里有教练、分析师，甚至还有体能训练师。健康的身体和清醒的头脑能帮助选手发挥得更好。随着技术的进步，电子竞技可能会比许多传统运动更受欢迎。

For a beginner, trying esports can be fun and challenging. You do not need to be a young person to enjoy playing. But it is important to balance screen time with outdoor exercise and enough sleep. Watching top players can teach you useful strategies, but you should also learn to enjoy friendly matches. In the end, esports are one more way to connect with people around the world.

对初学者来说，尝试电子竞技既有趣又具有挑战性。你不必是年轻人才能享受游戏。但重要的是要平衡屏幕时间与户外运动以及充足的睡眠。观看顶级选手可以教你一些有用的策略，但你也应该学会享受友好的比赛。最终，电子竞技是连接世界各地人们的另一种方式。
