Working in Two Places: Home and the Office

Date: 2026-10-01 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Work and jobs — hybrid work

先通读一遍英文，猜大意；再对照中文，最后重点记 5 个短语和 5 个难词。

In many cities, the way people work has changed a lot. Before, most office workers travelled to the same building every morning. Now, many companies offer a hybrid plan. This means employees spend some days at home and some days in the office. It gives people more freedom, but it also brings new questions.

在很多城市，人们工作的方式已经发生了很大变化。以前，大多数上班族每天早上都去同一栋楼上班。现在，许多公司提供混合办公方案。这意味着员工有些天在家工作，有些天在办公室工作。这给人们更多自由，但也带来了新问题。

There are good sides to this style. Workers save time and money because they do not need to commute. They can also focus better at home. However, some people feel lonely without colleagues nearby. New staff may find it hard to learn from others when they work alone.

这种工作方式有好处。员工节省了时间和金钱，因为他们不需要通勤。他们在家也能更专注。不过，有些人因为身边没有同事而感到孤单。新员工独自工作时，可能觉得很难向别人学习。

If you have a hybrid job, try to stay organised. Keep a clear plan for each week and talk with your team often. When you go to the office, use that time to meet people and share ideas. A good balance can make your work life happier and more productive.

如果你做混合办公的工作，尽量保持有条理。为每周制定清晰的计划，并经常和团队沟通。去办公室时，利用那段时间见见人、分享想法。良好的平衡能让你的工作生活更快乐、更高效。
