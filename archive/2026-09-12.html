Answering "Tell Me About Yourself"

Date: 2026-09-12 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Job Interviews and Career Skills

面试回答要简短有结构：现在—过去—未来，控制在两分钟内，并多练习。

Imagine you are sitting in an interview. The interviewer smiles and asks, &quot;Tell me about yourself.&quot; This simple question makes many people nervous. However, it is also your best chance to make a good first impression. A clear and short answer can open the door to more questions.

想象你正坐在面试中。面试官微笑着问："请介绍一下你自己。"这个简单的问题让很多人紧张。不过，这也是你留下良好第一印象的最好机会。一个清晰简短的回答能带来更多提问。

Experts suggest a simple structure. First, talk about your present role or study. Second, share one or two past experiences that show your skills. Third, explain why you want this new job. Keep your answer under two minutes, and connect your words to the company&#39;s needs.

专家建议用一个简单的结构。第一，谈谈你现在的职位或学习。第二，分享一两个能展示你技能的过去经历。第三，说明你为什么想要这份新工作。把回答控制在两分钟以内，并把你的话和公司的需求联系起来。

Practice is the key. Record yourself, or ask a friend to listen. Then improve your tone and speed. Remember, you do not need perfect English. You need to sound calm, honest and ready to learn. Good preparation gives you real confidence.

练习是关键。录下自己，或者请朋友听。然后改进你的语气和语速。记住，你不需要完美的英语。你需要听起来冷静、诚实并愿意学习。充分的准备会带给你真正的自信。
