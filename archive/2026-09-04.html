La Tomatina: A Strange and Fun Celebration

Date: 2026-09-04 | Level: Beginner | Topic: La Tomatina (西班牙番茄节)

In the small town of Buñol, Spain, there is a very unusual festival called La Tomatina. It takes place every year in the late summer, usually in August. Thousands of people come from many countries to join this event. On that day, the streets are full of laughter, music, and red tomatoes.

在西班牙的小镇布尼奥尔，有一个非常特别的节日，叫“番茄节”。它每年夏末举行，通常在八月。数以千计的人从世界各地前来参加这个活动。那一天，街道上充满了笑声、音乐和红色的番茄。

The main activity is a huge tomato fight. Trucks move through the streets and bring piles of soft, ripe tomatoes. People throw them at each other, not to hurt, but to have fun. After about one hour, everyone stops. Then they use small machines to wash the streets and their bodies with clean water.

主要活动是一场巨大的“丢番茄大战”。卡车穿过街道，运来一筐筐柔软又熟透的番茄。人们互相投掷番茄，不是为了伤害对方，而是为了好玩。大约一个小时后，大家都停下来。然后人们用机器，用干净的水冲洗街道和自己的身体。

La Tomatina began in 1945, but it became very popular over time. Today it is a symbol of joy and letting go of stress. People must follow some rules, like crushing tomatoes before throwing them to keep everyone safe. For many visitors, the festival is a great way to remember that life can be colorful, messy, and joyful.

番茄节始于 1945 年，后来变得越来越受欢迎。如今，它象征着欢乐和释放压力。人们必须遵守一些规则，比如在扔番茄之前先把它捏碎，以保护大家的安全。对许多游客来说，这个节日是一个很好的提醒：生活可以五彩缤纷、乱乱糟槽，但依然充满快乐。
