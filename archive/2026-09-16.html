The First Hot Air Balloon

Date: 2026-09-16 | Level: Beginner | Topic: A simple history story — the first hot air balloon flight

先读英文一遍抓大意，再看中文确认细节；注意 past tense（过去式）动词。

In 1783, two French brothers, Joseph and Étienne Montgolfier, discovered something surprising. When they held a paper bag over a fire, hot air filled it and lifted it upward. They built a large balloon from paper and cloth. They did not know why hot air rose, but they believed it could carry people into the sky.

1783年，法国两兄弟约瑟夫和艾蒂安·蒙戈尔菲耶发现了一件令人惊讶的事。当他们把纸袋放在火上时，热空气充满袋子，把它向上托起。他们用纸和布做了一个大热气球。他们不知道热空气为什么会上升，但他们相信它能载人上天。

Before sending a person, they tested the balloon with animals. In September 1783, a sheep, a duck, and a rooster flew above a crowd in France. The flight lasted about eight minutes, and all three animals landed safely. This success gave the brothers courage to continue.

在送人上天之前，他们先用动物测试热气球。1783年9月，一只羊、一只鸭和一只公鸡在法国一群人头顶上飞过。飞行持续了大约八分钟，三只动物都安全落地。这次成功给了兄弟俩继续下去的勇气。

Two months later, two brave men stood in a basket under a balloon and rose above Paris. They travelled for about twenty-five minutes and floated over the rooftops. Crowds below cheered loudly. Soon, ballooning became a popular hobby across Europe, and people today still enjoy it at festivals.

两个月后，两名勇敢的男子站在热气球下的吊篮里，升上了巴黎上空。他们飞行了大约二十五分钟，飘过屋顶。下面的人群大声欢呼。很快，乘热气球成为欧洲各地流行的爱好，如今人们仍喜欢在节日里乘坐它。
