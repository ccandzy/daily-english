How Social Media Decides What You See Next

Date: 2026-11-06 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: Algorithms and social media recommendations — how a recommendation system chooses your next video

阅读时先抓住“它想让你多停留”这个核心，再看平台如何用你的行为做判断。

When you open a short-video app, you do not see a random list. A recommendation system chooses each video for you. It watches what you do: do you stop, watch to the end, or swipe away quickly? These small actions become signals. If you watch cooking videos for a long time, the system thinks, "This person likes cooking." Then it shows you more cooking videos. It is not reading your mind. It is simply learning from your past behavior.

当你打开一个短视频应用时，你看到的并不是随机的列表。推荐系统会为你挑选每一个视频。它会观察你的行为：你是停下来、看到最后，还是很快划走？这些小动作都会变成信号。如果你长时间看做饭视频，系统就会想：“这个人喜欢做饭。”然后它给你看更多做饭视频。它并不是在读取你的想法，它只是从你过去的行为中学习。

But there is a trade-off. The system wants you to stay longer, because longer time means more ads and more money. So it often chooses videos that are exciting or easy to watch. This can be helpful: you find new things you enjoy. But it can also trap you in a small bubble. You see the same kind of content again and again. You may miss other topics you would also like. The app is good at giving you more of the same, not at showing you something truly new.

但这里有一个取舍。系统想让你停留更久，因为时间越长，广告越多，赚的钱也越多。所以它常常选择刺激或容易观看的视频。这可能有帮助：你能发现自己喜欢的新东西。但它也可能把你困在一个小圈子里。你一遍又一遍地看到同类内容。你可能会错过其他你也会喜欢的主题。这个应用擅长给你更多相同的东西，却不擅长给你真正新的东西。

So how can you use this knowledge? First, notice your own habits. If your feed feels boring or too similar, change your behavior. Watch a different topic for a few minutes, or search for something new. Second, remember that the app is not your friend; it is a machine following a goal. You can still enjoy it, but you should decide what you want to watch. A simple rule: ask yourself, "Did I choose this video, or did the app choose it for me?"

那么你该如何利用这个知识呢？首先，注意你自己的习惯。如果你的信息流感觉无聊或太相似，就改变你的行为。花几分钟看一个不同的主题，或者搜索一些新东西。其次，记住这个应用不是你的朋友；它是一台追随某个目标的机器。你仍然可以享受它，但你应该决定自己想看什么。一个简单的规则：问问自己，“是我选了这个视频，还是应用替我选的？”
