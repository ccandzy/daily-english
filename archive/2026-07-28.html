The Growing Popularity of E-books and Audiobooks

Date: 2025-03-16 | Level: Beginner | Topic: books and reading

在本课中，你将学习关于电子书和有声书流行的词汇和表达。

Many people now choose to read e-books or listen to audiobooks instead of paper books. This is very convenient because you can carry hundreds of books on one device. Audiobooks also allow you to enjoy stories while doing other activities, like driving or cooking.

现在许多人选择阅读电子书或听有声书，而不是纸质书。这非常方便，因为你可以在一台设备上携带数百本书。有声书还让你在做其他事情时享受故事，比如开车或做饭。

E-books are often cheaper and easier to find than paper books. You can adjust the text size and quickly look up the meaning of difficult words. For language learners, audiobooks are especially helpful because you can hear correct pronunciation and follow along with the text.

电子书通常比纸质书更便宜，更容易找到。你可以调整文字大小并快速查找难词的意思。对于语言学习者来说，有声书特别有帮助，因为你可以听到正确的发音并跟随文本。

Despite the rise of digital reading, many people still love paper books. The best format depends on your lifestyle. Reading in any form — paper, digital, or audio — is good for your mind and helps you learn.

尽管数字阅读兴起，许多人仍然喜爱纸质书。最佳格式取决于你的生活方式。任何形式的阅读——纸质、数字或有声——都对大脑有益，并帮助你学习。
