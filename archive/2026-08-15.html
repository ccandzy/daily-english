Trees Make Cities Cooler and Healthier

Date: 2026-08-15 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Urban trees and their environmental benefits

先读英文段落，尝试理解大意，再对照中文翻译，最后复习短语和生词。

Trees are very important for life in cities. In summer, streets without trees become very hot because concrete and buildings hold heat. Trees give shade and release water into the air. This natural process helps lower the temperature around them. In recent years, record heat has become a serious problem. Many cities are now planting more trees to fight extreme heat.

树木对城市生活非常重要。在夏天，没有树木的街道会变得非常炎热，因为混凝土和建筑物会保留热量。树木提供阴凉并把水分释放到空气中。这一自然过程有助于降低周围温度。近年来，创纪录的高温已成为一个严重问题。许多城市现在正种植更多树木来应对极端高温。

Trees also clean the air. They catch dust and absorb harmful gases. A neighbourhood with many trees feels quieter and more peaceful. Spending time near trees can lower stress and improve mood. Scientists say that caring for green spaces is good for both the planet and people. Moreover, trees provide homes for birds and small animals, which makes city life richer.

树木还能净化空气。它们能阻挡灰尘并吸收有害气体。一个有许多树木的社区会感觉更安静、更平和。在树木附近度过时间可以减轻压力并改善情绪。科学家说，照料绿色空间对地球和人类都有好处。此外，树木为鸟类和小动物提供家园，这让城市生活更加丰富。

People can help by planting trees in their gardens or supporting local parks. Simple actions like watering young trees make a big difference. If every community adds a few trees, the future will be cooler and greener. Trees are a simple solution to a big problem. We do not need to wait for perfect conditions; we can start today, one tree at a time.

人们可以通过在花园里种树或支持当地公园来帮忙。像给幼树浇水这样的简单行动会产生很大影响。如果每个社区都增加几棵树，未来将会更凉爽、更绿色。树木是解决大问题的一种简单方法。我们不需要等待完美的条件；我们可以从今天开始，一次一棵树。
