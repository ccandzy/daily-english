Weather and Seasons Around Us

Date: 2026-09-22 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Weather and seasons

读的时候注意季节词汇和天气形容词，试着用它们描述你所在城市的今天。

Every season brings a different kind of weather, and people often say that no two days feel exactly the same. In spring, gentle rain falls quietly and new leaves appear on the trees. Summer usually arrives with strong sunlight, so many families stay near water or under the shade. Autumn feels cooler, and the wind carries dry leaves across the streets.

每个季节都带来不同的天气，人们常说没有两天感觉完全一样。春天，细雨静静落下，树上长出新的叶子。夏天通常伴着强烈的阳光到来，所以许多家庭待在水边或阴凉处。秋天感觉更凉爽，风把干枯的叶子吹过街道。

Winter can be hard for some people because the days are short and the air feels cold. However, cold weather also brings simple pleasures, such as hot soup, warm coats, and quiet evenings at home. When snow covers the ground, children often go outside to play, while adults prefer to watch from a window.

冬天对一些人来说可能很难熬，因为白天短，空气也冷。不过，寒冷的天气也带来简单的快乐，比如热汤、暖和的外套，以及在家安静的夜晚。当雪覆盖地面时，孩子们常常出去玩，而大人更喜欢从窗户里看着。

Nowadays, many people check the weather report on their phones before leaving home. This small habit helps us choose the right clothes and plan our day more carefully. Even if the forecast is not always correct, it reminds us to prepare. Whatever the season is, we can enjoy it in our own way.

如今，许多人在出门前会用手机查看天气预报。这个小小的习惯帮助我们选择合适的衣服，更仔细地安排一天。即使预报不总是准确，它也提醒我们做好准备。无论是什么季节，我们都能以自己的方式享受它。
