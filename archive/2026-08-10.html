Why City Parks Are Good for Us

Date: 2026-08-10 | Level: Beginner | Topic: nature and outdoors / urban green spaces

城市公园就像大自然的缩影，多去走走有益健康。

City parks are like small green islands in busy cities. They have trees, grass, and sometimes a small lake. Many people go there to relax, walk, or play with their children. Parks are not just for fun. They also help clean the air and cool the city in summer.

城市公园就像繁忙城市中的绿色小岛。公园里有树木、草地，有时还有小湖。许多人去那里放松、散步，或者和孩子一起玩。公园不只是为了好玩。它们还能帮助净化空气，并在夏天给城市降温。

Walking in a park is a simple way to feel happier. When you leave your phone at home and look at the green trees, your mind becomes calmer. Studies show that spending time outdoors can lower stress and make your heart stronger. Even ten minutes in a park can change your mood.

在公园里散步是一种让人更开心的简单方法。当你把手机留在家中，看着绿色的树木，你的思绪会变得更平静。研究表明，花时间在户外可以减轻压力，让心脏更强健。即使是在公园里待十分钟也能改变你的心情。

In many cities, people are planting more trees and making new parks. They know that nature is important for a healthy life. You can enjoy nature too, even if you live in a big city. Try to visit a park this week. Your body and mind will thank you.

在许多城市，人们正在种植更多的树木，建设新的公园。他们知道自然对健康生活很重要。即使你住在大城市，也可以享受自然。这周试着去一次公园吧。你的身体和心灵会感谢你。
