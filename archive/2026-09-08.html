Why Cities Need More Green Spaces

Date: 2026-09-08 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Urban Green Spaces and the Environment

读英语时先抓住每段主题句，再留意与环保相关的常用搭配。

City life is often noisy and full of concrete. In modern towns, people might not notice how much they need nature. Parks and green areas are not simply places for exercise or picnics. Trees and plants take in carbon dioxide and release oxygen, so they help clean the air. On hot days, green spaces also make the temperature more comfortable.

城市生活常常嘈杂又忙碌，而且到处都是混凝土建筑。在现代城镇，人们可能没有注意到自己有多需要自然。公园和绿地不只是锻炼或野餐的地方。树木和植物吸入二氧化碳并释放氧气，因此能帮助清洁空气。在炎热的日子里，绿地也能让气温更舒适。

Rooftop gardens and small community parks are becoming popular. These green spaces can absorb rainwater, so heavy rain is less likely to cause flooding. People also plant vegetables and flowers together in shared gardens. These places attract birds and bees, improve local food supplies, and give neighbours a quiet spot to meet.

屋顶花园和小型社区公园正越来越受欢迎。这些绿地能吸收雨水，所以下大雨时就不太容易造成水灾。人们还在共享花园里一起种植蔬菜和花卉。这些地方能吸引鸟类和蜜蜂，改善本地食物供应，并给邻居提供一个安静的见面场所。

Some people think that building parks costs too much, but green space is a smart investment. As cities grow, they need room for homes, shops, and roads. However, planners should keep some land for nature. If residents join the planning process, new communities can have both modern buildings and trees. In the end, a green city gives us cleaner air, better health, and a happier life.

有些人认为建公园花费太高，但绿地是一项明智的投资。随着城市发展，我们需要空间来盖住宅、商店和道路。然而，规划者也应该留一些土地给自然。如果居民参与规划过程，新社区就能同时拥有现代建筑和树木。最终，绿色城市带给我们更清新的空气、更好的健康和更幸福的生活。
