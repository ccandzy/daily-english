Study Better with Short Breaks

Date: 2026-08-25 | Level: Beginner | Topic: education and study tips

短时间学习加休息，效果更好。

Many students think that long hours are the only way to learn. But recent research shows that short, focused study sessions work better. When you study in 25-minute blocks and then take a 5-minute break, your brain stays fresh and alert. This simple method is called the Pomodoro Technique.

许多学生认为长时间学习是唯一的方法。但最近的研究表明，短而专注的学习时段效果更好。当你以25分钟为单位学习，然后休息5分钟，你的大脑保持清醒和警觉。这个简单的方法叫做番茄工作法。

The key is to remove all distractions during your study block. Put your phone away and sit at a clean desk with only your books. After each session, stand up, stretch, or look out the window. These small breaks help you remember more without feeling tired or bored.

关键在于在学习时段内排除所有干扰。把手机放好，坐在只有书本的整洁书桌前。每次学习后，站起来、伸展一下，或者看看窗外。这些短暂的休息帮助你记住更多，而不感到疲惫或无聊。

You can start with just two or three blocks a day. As you get used to it, add more sessions slowly. Remember, quality is more important than quantity. A calm and focused mind learns much better than a tired one. Try it tomorrow and see the difference.

你可以从每天只有两三个时段开始。当你习惯后，慢慢增加更多时段。记住，质量比数量更重要。平静而专注的头脑比疲惫的头脑学得好得多。明天试试吧，看看有什么不同。
