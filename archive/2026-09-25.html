Small Robots, Big Discoveries

Date: 2026-09-25 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Ocean science discoveries with small robots

先读英文，再对照中文，注意动词和常用搭配。

Scientists are exploring the deep ocean in a new way. They use small robots that can swim on their own. These robots are about the size of a football. They can go down thousands of meters. There, the water is dark and very cold. But the robots can take photos and measure the water. They help scientists learn about animals far below the surface.

科学家正在用一种新方式探索深海。他们使用能自行游动的小型机器人。这些机器人约有一个足球那么大。它们能下潜数千米。那里的水又黑又冷。但机器人可以拍照并测量水质。它们帮助科学家了解海面以下的动物。

Last year, a team sent several robots to study a deep sea mountain. The robots found warm water coming from the rocks. This water is full of minerals. Many small creatures live near it. The team was surprised by the number of different species. Some may be new to science. The robots also sent videos back to the ship.

去年，一个团队派出几个机器人研究一座深海山。机器人发现岩石中冒出温水。这种水富含矿物质。许多小生物生活在附近。团队对不同的物种数量感到惊讶。有些可能是科学新发现。机器人还把视频传回船上。

This work is slow, but exciting. The ocean covers most of our planet, yet we know little about it. Every robot trip can bring a small discovery. That discovery may teach us how life survives in hard places. It may also help us care for the sea. Who knows what the next robot will find?

这项工作很慢，但令人兴奋。海洋覆盖了我们星球的大部分，但我们对其知之甚少。每次机器人出行都可能带来一个小发现。这个发现可能教会我们生命如何在艰难环境中生存。它也可能帮助我们保护海洋。谁知道下一个机器人会发现什么呢？
