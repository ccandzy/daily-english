Urban Gardens: Greening Our Cities

Date: 2026-07-27 | Level: Beginner | Topic: urban gardening and environmental benefits

本课探讨城市园艺对环境的积极影响，学习相关词汇和表达。

More people in cities are starting small gardens on rooftops and balconies. This helps them grow fresh vegetables and herbs. Urban gardens also make cities cooler and cleaner. They are a simple way to enjoy nature and help the environment.

越来越多的城市居民在屋顶和阳台上开始种植小花园。这帮助他们种植新鲜的蔬菜和香草。城市花园也让城市更凉爽、更干净。它们是享受自然和帮助环境的简单方式。

These gardens reduce the amount of carbon dioxide in the air. Plants absorb CO2 and release oxygen. They also lower temperatures in hot summer months. In addition, urban gardens provide homes for bees and other insects.

这些花园减少了空气中的二氧化碳含量。植物吸收二氧化碳并释放氧气。它们还在炎热的夏季降低温度。此外，城市花园为蜜蜂和其他昆虫提供了家园。

Starting an urban garden is easy. You only need a few pots, soil, and seeds. You can grow tomatoes, lettuce, or flowers. Even a small space can become a green corner. Urban gardening brings joy and helps our planet.

开始一个城市花园很简单。你只需要几个花盆、土壤和种子。你可以种植西红柿、生菜或花卉。即使狭小的空间也能变成绿色角落。城市花园带来快乐，并帮助我们的地球。

This lesson is based on general knowledge about urban gardening and its environmental benefits.
