When Machines Take Over Tasks, Which Skills Should Workers Learn?

Date: 2026-11-13 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: Automation and changing careers — choosing which skills to build when machines take over routine tasks

阅读时先找出“机器做什么”和“人做什么”的对比，这能帮你抓住文章的核心逻辑。

Many offices now use software to do routine work. The software can sort emails, check simple forms, and answer common customer questions. This saves time, but some workers worry about their jobs. A bank clerk may find that a computer now handles most daily records. The question is not whether machines can do tasks. The real question is which tasks still need a person. Usually, these are tasks that need judgment, trust, or talking with people.

现在很多办公室用软件来做日常工作。软件可以整理邮件、检查简单的表格，还能回答常见的客户问题。这节省了时间，但一些员工担心自己的工作。一位银行职员可能发现，现在电脑处理了大部分日常记录。问题不在于机器能不能完成任务，真正的问题是哪些任务仍然需要人来做。通常，这些是需要判断力、信任或与人交谈的任务。

So when your job changes, do not try to learn everything. Instead, look for one skill that machines do poorly. For example, a worker whose factory job becomes more automated might learn to explain problems clearly to customers. Or a clerk might learn to lead a short team meeting. These skills are hard to automate because they depend on people. The trade-off is simple: learning a big new subject takes years, but learning one people-focused skill can start this month.

所以当你的工作发生变化时，不要试图学习所有东西。相反，去找一项机器做不好的技能。例如，一个工厂工作变得更自动化的员工，可以学习如何向客户清楚地解释问题。或者一位职员可以学习主持一个简短的团队会议。这些技能很难自动化，因为它们依赖于人。权衡很简单：学习一门大的新学科需要多年，但学习一项以人为中心的技能这个月就可以开始。

You can test this idea without quitting your job. First, choose one skill, like giving clear instructions. Next, practice it in a small real situation at work. Then, ask a colleague for honest feedback. If people say you are getting better, you have proof that the skill is useful. This small step lowers the risk before any big career move. Machines may take over tasks, but people who can guide, explain, and support others will still find work.

你可以在不辞职的情况下测试这个想法。首先，选择一项技能，比如给出清晰的指示。接着，在工作中一个小的真实场景里练习它。然后，请同事给你诚实的反馈。如果人们说你正在进步，你就有了这项技能有用的证据。这个小小的步骤降低了任何重大职业变动之前的风险。机器可能会接管任务，但能够指导、解释和支持他人的人仍然会找到工作。

Grammar

语法小课堂

一般现在时表示普遍事实

文中原句：This saves time, but some workers worry about their jobs.

结构：Subject + verb (present simple) + object

说明：一般现在时用来描述经常发生的情况或普遍事实。这里说明软件带来的常见结果和员工的普遍担忧。

仿写：This app saves money, but some people worry about privacy.

这个应用省钱，但一些人担心隐私。

情态动词 may 表示可能性

文中原句：Machines may take over tasks, but people who can guide, explain, and support others will still find work.

结构：Subject + may + verb + ...

说明：may 表示“可能”，用来谈论将来可能发生的事。这里说明机器接管任务是可能的情况，但并非绝对。

仿写：The weather may change, but we will still go out.

天气可能会变，但我们仍然会出门。
