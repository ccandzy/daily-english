Moving Around a Busy City

Date: 2026-09-21 | Level: Beginner | Topic: City life and transport (non-electric)

学习交通词汇时，注意常用搭配，如 catch a bus, get off, traffic rules。

In many big cities, people use different ways to travel every day. Some walk to work, some ride bicycles, and others take buses. During rush hour, streets can be very busy. You may see long lines of cars, taxis, and motorcycles. Sometimes the fastest way is simply to walk.

在许多大城市，人们每天用不同方式出行。有些人走路上班，有些人骑自行车，还有些人坐公交车。高峰时段，街道会非常繁忙。你可能会看到汽车、出租车和摩托车排成长队。有时最快的办法就是走路。

Buses are useful for longer trips. You need to check the route and buy a ticket before you go. In some cities, you can pay with a card or a phone. When the bus is full, you might stand for a while. Try to get off at the right stop, or you may travel too far.

公交车对较长路程很有用。出发前你需要查路线并买票。在一些城市，你可以用卡或手机支付。公交车满员时，你可能要站一会儿。尽量在正确的车站下车，否则你可能会坐过站。

Riding a bicycle is cheap and healthy. Many cities now have special lanes for bikes. However, you must be careful with cars and people. Always use lights at night and follow traffic rules. A short bike ride can save time and make your day more enjoyable.

骑自行车既便宜又健康。许多城市现在有专门的自行车道。不过，你必须小心汽车和行人。晚上一定要使用车灯，并遵守交通规则。短途骑车可以节省时间，让你的日子更愉快。
