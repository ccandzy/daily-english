城市如何应对越来越热的夏天

Date: 2026-11-14 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: Climate adaptation and extreme weather — how cities use shade and water to cope with hotter summers

阅读时先找出“问题—办法—效果”的结构，这能帮助你更快理解说明类文章。

Many cities now have hotter summers than before. On some days, the temperature stays high even at night. This is hard for older people and for people who work outside. In a hot city, some streets are much hotter than others. Streets with many trees are cooler. Streets with only concrete and cars can be very hot. This is because trees give shade and water from their leaves. Shade keeps people and buildings cooler. So some city planners now plant more trees on busy streets.

现在许多城市的夏天比以前更热。有些日子，即使到了晚上，气温仍然很高。这对老年人和在户外工作的人来说很难熬。在一个炎热的城市里，有些街道比另一些街道热得多。种了很多树的街道更凉快。只有水泥和汽车的街道可能非常热。这是因为树木提供阴凉，叶子还会释放水分。阴凉让人和建筑更凉快。所以一些城市规划者现在在繁忙的街道上种更多树。

But planting trees is not always easy. A young tree needs years to grow big enough to give good shade. It also needs water, and in a dry summer water can be expensive. Some streets are too narrow for big trees. In these places, cities can use other ideas. They can build simple roofs over bus stops. They can paint roofs white so they reflect sunlight. They can also add small water fountains in public squares. These ideas work faster than trees, but they cost money to build and to keep clean.

但是种树并不总是容易的。一棵小树需要很多年才能长得足够大，提供好的阴凉。它也需要水，而在干旱的夏天，水可能很贵。有些街道太窄，种不了大树。在这些地方，城市可以用其他办法。他们可以在公交站上方建简单的顶棚。他们可以把屋顶刷成白色，让屋顶反射阳光。他们还可以在公共广场加小喷泉。这些办法比种树见效快，但建造和保持清洁都需要花钱。

So what is the best choice? Many experts say cities should use both trees and quick fixes. Trees are slow, but they last for many years and also help birds and insects. Quick fixes like white roofs and bus stop roofs help people right away. A good plan starts with the hottest streets and the people who need help most, such as older people and outdoor workers. Then the city can plant trees for the future and add shade now. The lesson is simple: there is no single answer, but small steps together can make summer safer.

那么最好的选择是什么呢？许多专家说，城市应该同时使用树木和快速解决办法。树木见效慢，但能持续很多年，还能帮助鸟类和昆虫。白色屋顶和公交站顶棚这样的快速办法能立刻帮助人们。好的计划从最热的街道和最需要帮助的人开始，比如老年人和户外工作者。然后城市可以为未来种树，同时现在就增加阴凉。这个道理很简单：没有单一的答案，但小步骤加在一起可以让夏天更安全。

Grammar

语法小课堂

比较级（than）

文中原句：Many cities now have hotter summers than before.

结构：A + be/动词 + 比较级 + than + B

说明：用比较级加 than 比较两者，说明一方程度更高。注意 hot 变 hotter 要双写辅音。

仿写：This street is cooler than that one.

这条街比那条街更凉快。

because 引导原因状语从句

文中原句：This is because trees give shade and water from their leaves.

结构：This is because + 主语 + 动词 + 其他

说明：because 用来说明原因，回答“为什么”。This is because… 常用于解释前面提到的现象。

仿写：The bus is late because the road is busy.

公交车晚点了，因为路上很忙。

不定式作目的（to + 动词原形）

文中原句：A young tree needs years to grow big enough to give good shade.

结构：主语 + 动词 + to + 动词原形

说明：to + 动词原形可以表示目的或结果。这里 to grow 表示树需要多年“去长大”。

仿写：We plant trees to make the street cooler.

我们种树是为了让街道更凉快。
