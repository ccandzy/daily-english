Sleep: A Secret Study Tip

Date: 2026-09-11 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Education and study tips

学习不只是"多花时间"，睡眠也在帮你整理记忆。读的时候注意每段的因果连接词。

Many students study late at night before an important exam. They believe that more hours always mean better scores. Scientists, however, say that sleep is a real part of learning, not a waste of time. In fact, studying while exhausted can even hurt your memory.

许多学生在重要考试前熬夜学习。他们相信时间越长，分数就越高。然而，科学家说睡眠是学习的真正一部分，并不是浪费时间。事实上，在精疲力尽时学习甚至会损害你的记忆。

During deep sleep, your brain repeats the new information you met during the day. It moves important facts into long-term memory and throws away useless details. This explains why a short review before bed often works surprisingly well. The brain is busy organizing your knowledge while you sleep quietly.

在深度睡眠中，你的大脑会重复你白天接触的新信息。它把重要的事实存入长期记忆，并丢掉无用的细节。这就解释了为什么睡前短暂复习往往效果出奇地好。当你安静睡觉时，大脑正忙着整理你的知识。

So try a simple habit this week. Review your notes for ten minutes in the evening, then sleep for seven or eight hours. In the morning, test yourself without looking at the book. Repeat this routine for several days, and you may remember much more than you expect.

所以这周试试一个简单的习惯。晚上用十分钟复习笔记，然后睡七到八个小时。早上先不要看书，测试一下自己。坚持这个习惯几天，你可能会记得比想象中多得多。
