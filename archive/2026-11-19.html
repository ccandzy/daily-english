Why Old City Homes Stay Cool in Summer

Date: 2026-11-19 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: architecture and the way people live — how traditional courtyard homes stay cool without air conditioning

阅读时先找出文章里的“对比”（老房子 vs 新房子），这能帮你更快抓住重点。

In many old Chinese cities, you can still find traditional courtyard homes. These houses are built around a small open square in the middle. In summer, this square stays cool in the evening. The walls are thick, and the windows are small. Thick walls keep the hot sun outside during the day. Small windows let in less heat. People also plant a tree in the courtyard. The tree gives shade and makes the air feel fresher. So the family can sit outside and enjoy the cool evening without using a fan.

在许多中国老城里，你仍然能找到传统的四合院。这些房子围绕中间一个小露天院子建造。夏天，这个院子在傍晚很凉快。墙很厚，窗户很小。厚墙在白天把炎热的太阳挡在外面。小窗户让更少的热量进来。人们还会在院子里种一棵树。树提供阴凉，让空气感觉更清爽。所以一家人可以坐在外面，享受凉爽的傍晚，而不用开风扇。

Modern apartments work in a very different way. They often have large windows and thin walls. Large windows let in a lot of light, which is nice in winter. But in summer, the same windows let in a lot of heat. Then people must turn on the air conditioner. This uses electricity and costs money. It also makes some people feel uncomfortable. So a design that is good for one season can be bad for another. The old courtyard home solves the summer problem with shape and shade, not with machines.

现代公寓的运作方式很不一样。它们通常有大窗户和薄墙。大窗户让很多光线进来，这在冬天很好。但在夏天，同样的窗户也让很多热量进来。于是人们必须开空调。这要用电，也要花钱。它还会让一些人觉得不舒服。所以，对一个季节好的设计，可能对另一个季节不好。老四合院用形状和阴凉解决夏天的问题，而不是用机器。

This does not mean we should all move back to old houses. Old homes can be dark, and they may not fit modern families. But they teach us a simple lesson. Before we buy a big air conditioner, we can think about the building itself. A tree outside a window, a thick curtain, or a small shade can make a room cooler. These small changes cost little and help the planet. Good design often starts with watching how a house meets the sun and the wind.

这并不意味着我们都应该搬回老房子。老房子可能很暗，也可能不适合现代家庭。但它们教给我们一个简单的道理。在买一台大空调之前，我们可以先想想建筑本身。窗外的一棵树、一幅厚窗帘，或者一小块遮阳，都能让房间更凉快。这些小改变花钱少，也对地球有好处。好的设计常常始于观察一座房子如何面对阳光和风。

Grammar

语法小课堂

一般现在时（描述普遍事实）

文中原句：Thick walls keep the hot sun outside during the day.

结构：主语 + 动词原形/动词-s + 其他

说明：用来描述习惯、规律或普遍事实。主语是第三人称单数时，动词要加 -s。

仿写：Small windows keep the room cool in summer.

小窗户在夏天让房间保持凉爽。

情态动词 can 表示能力或可能

文中原句：So the family can sit outside and enjoy the cool evening without using a fan.

结构：主语 + can + 动词原形 + 其他

说明：can 后面直接跟动词原形，表示“能够”或“可以”。否定形式是 cannot / can't。

仿写：You can open the window to let in fresh air.

你可以打开窗户让新鲜空气进来。
