Working from Home: The New Normal

Date: 2026-10-23 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Hybrid work and modern jobs

阅读时先抓住每段的主题句，再回看细节；注意 “both...and...” 和 “between...and...” 的搭配。

Many offices changed after 2020, and the way people work is still changing. Now some employees go to the office only two or three days a week. This is called hybrid work. Companies say it saves money, and workers feel less tired because they do not travel every day.

2020年后，许多办公室发生了变化，人们的工作方式仍在改变。现在有些员工每周只去办公室两三天。这被称为混合办公。公司说这样省钱，员工因为不用每天通勤，也没那么累。

But hybrid work brings problems too. Some managers worry that teamwork becomes weaker when people are not in the same room. New staff may find it hard to learn from experienced colleagues. Others say they work longer hours at home and cannot stop in the evening.

但混合办公也带来问题。一些经理担心，大家不在同一间屋里时，团队合作会变弱。新员工可能觉得很难向有经验的同事学习。还有人说在家工作时间更长，晚上也停不下来。

Experts suggest clear rules. Teams should agree on which days they meet in person, so nobody feels left out. Managers should ask how everyone feels each week. If people plan carefully, hybrid work can be good for both the company and the staff. It can also help workers keep a better balance between job and life.

专家建议制定明确的规则。团队应商定哪几天线下见面，这样没人会觉得被排除在外。经理应每周询问大家的感受。如果计划得仔细，混合办公对公司和对员工都有好处。它还能帮员工在工作和生活之间保持更好的平衡。
