Exercise Habits: Start Small, Stay Steady

Date: 2026-10-29 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: exercise habits and long-term motivation — using a fixed signal instead of waiting to feel ready

学习建议：把“想不想练”换成固定的开始信号，先做几分钟，再决定是否加量。

On Monday, Mei plans to run after work. By six o’clock, she feels tired, so she sits down. The plan is good, but her motivation is low. This is common. Motivation is the feeling that you want to do something. It changes from day to day, like the weather. A long-term exercise habit cannot depend on this feeling every time.

周一，梅计划下班后去跑步。到了六点，她觉得很累，就坐下了。这个计划很好，但她的动力很低。这很常见。动力就是你想要做某事的感受。它像天气一样，天天都会变。长期的运动习惯不能每次都靠这种感觉。

A simple answer is to use a signal and a small routine. A signal is a clear sign, such as after breakfast or before your evening shower. Put your shoes near the door. Then do a short session, even five minutes. The routine should be easy to start, because a small start needs less effort. You can still do more when you feel good.

一个简单的办法是使用一个信号和一个小常规。信号是一个清楚的提示，比如早餐后或晚上洗澡前。把鞋子放在门边。然后做一小段运动，哪怕只有五分钟。这个常规应该容易开始，因为小小的开始需要的力气更少。感觉好的时候，你仍然可以多做一点。

Waiting to feel ready is a trap. A useful balance is a fixed signal with a flexible amount. Do your short session on busy days, and a longer one on easy days. Over months, consistency matters more than one big effort. If you miss a day, do not quit. Just use the same signal again and get back on track. Your habit becomes more automatic.

等着自己“感觉准备好了”是一个陷阱。一个有用的平衡是：固定信号，弹性安排运动量。忙的日子做短的那次，轻松的日子做长一点。几个月下来，坚持比一次大努力更重要。如果漏了一天，不要放弃。只要再次使用同一个信号，回到正轨。你的习惯会变得更自动。
