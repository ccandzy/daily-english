Small Steps Make Learning Easier

Date: 2026-10-06 | Level: Beginner | Topic: city life and transport (non-electric)

今天先完成一次短阅读。稳定的小进步，比偶尔学习很久更容易坚持。

Learning English does not always require a long study session. Ten focused minutes can be useful when you read slowly, notice new expressions, and think about the meaning of each sentence.

学习英语并不总是需要很长的学习时间。慢慢阅读、留意新表达并思考每句话的含义时，专注的十分钟也很有用。

A simple daily routine can make progress easier to see. You may read one short article, choose several useful words, and then explain the main idea in your own language.

简单的每日习惯可以让进步更容易被看见。你可以读一篇短文，选择几个有用的单词，然后用自己的语言解释主要内容。

Some days will be busy, and your work may be incomplete. That is normal. The important thing is to return the next day and keep your learning habit alive.

有些日子会很忙，学习也可能没有全部完成。这很正常。重要的是第二天继续回来，让学习习惯保持下去。
