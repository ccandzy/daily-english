Why a Free Lesson Is Not Always Equal Learning

Date: 2026-10-28 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: education inequality and access to learning — a free online lesson and home support

阅读时先找“免费”和“真正能学”之间的差别，再用自己的话复述三个小步骤。

Two students get the same free online lesson. One has a quiet desk, a good phone, and a parent who can help. The other shares a room, uses a slow phone, and works late. The lesson is free for both, but their access to learning is not the same. The class may be equal, but the chance to use it is not. This is one small face of education inequality.

两名学生上同一节免费在线课。一个人有安静的书桌、好用的手机，还有能帮忙的家长。另一个人和别人合住一间房，用慢手机，还要很晚干活。这节课对两人都免费，但他们获得学习的机会并不一样。课堂也许平等，使用课堂的机会却不平等。这是教育不平等的一个小侧面。

Schools and free programs often measure access by counting places or lessons. However, learning also needs time, quiet, and support. A student who must cook, care for a younger sister, or travel far may miss the class. A free lesson can still cost the family time and energy. So, access is not just an open door. It is the whole path through that door.

学校和免费项目常常通过数名额或课程来衡量“可获得性”。然而，学习还需要时间、安静和支持。一个学生如果必须做饭、照顾妹妹，或走很远的路，就可能错过课程。一节免费课仍可能花掉家庭的时间和精力。所以，获得学习机会不只是打开一扇门。它是走完门后的整条路。

A simple takeaway is to ask what else a learner needs. A school can offer a quiet study room after class. A free program can lend tablets or share bus tickets. Families can take turns watching young children. These small steps do not fix inequality, but they help more students use the chance they already have. It is not about more lessons alone; it is about making the path easier.

一个简单的要点是：问问学习者还需要什么。学校可以在课后提供一间安静的自习室。免费项目可以借出平板电脑，或分担公交车票。家人可以轮流照看年幼的孩子。这些小事不能解决不平等，但能帮助更多学生用上已有的机会。这不只是多上几节课的问题，而是让这条路更容易走。
