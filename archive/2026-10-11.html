Cooking at Home: Simple and Smart

Date: 2026-10-11 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Food and Cooking — Home Cooking Habits

先读一遍英文，抓住大意，再看中文翻译检查理解。注意文中表示“好处”和“建议”的句子，它们最常用。

Many people are cooking at home more often these days. It is not only cheaper than eating in restaurants, but also healthier. When you prepare your own meals, you can control the salt, oil, and sugar. You also know exactly what goes into your food. For many families, cooking has become a relaxing hobby, not a boring duty.

如今许多人更常在家做饭。这不仅比在餐馆吃饭便宜，也更健康。自己做饭时，你能控制盐、油和糖的用量。你也很清楚食物里放了什么。对很多家庭来说，做饭已成为一种放松的爱好，而不是无聊的任务。

However, cooking every day can feel tiring after a long day at work. A smart solution is planning ahead. On weekends, wash and cut vegetables, cook some rice, and store everything in boxes. Then, on busy evenings, you can put a simple meal together in fifteen minutes. This habit saves both time and money during the week.

不过，工作一整天后，每天做饭可能会让人感到疲惫。一个聪明的办法就是提前计划。周末时洗好切好蔬菜，煮些米饭，把东西都装进盒子里。那么忙碌的晚上，你十五分钟就能凑出一顿简单的饭。这个习惯在工作日既省时间又省钱。

Simple dishes are often the best. For example, a bowl of tomato and egg noodles takes very little effort, yet it tastes warm and satisfying. Beginners should start with easy recipes and not worry about mistakes. Every time you cook, you learn something new, and your food slowly gets better. So do not be afraid to try.

简单的菜往往最好。比如，一碗西红柿鸡蛋面几乎不费力气，却温暖又让人满足。初学者应从简单的食谱开始，不必担心出错。每次做饭你都会学到新东西，你的厨艺也会慢慢变好。所以不要害怕尝试。
