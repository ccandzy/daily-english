The Edinburgh Festival Fringe: A City of Creativity

Date: 2026-08-19 | Level: Beginner | Topic: The Edinburgh Festival Fringe, an open-arts festival in Scotland

阅读提示：先看短语和单词，再读段落，更容易理解。

Every August, Edinburgh becomes a huge stage. The Edinburgh Festival Fringe is the world&#39;s largest arts festival. For three weeks, streets and buildings fill with performers. Actors, comedians, dancers, and musicians come from many countries. They perform in theatres, parks, and even small shops. Visitors can choose from thousands of shows.

每年八月，爱丁堡都会变成一个大舞台。爱丁堡边缘艺术节是世界上规模最大的艺术节。在三个星期里，街道和建筑里都挤满了表演者。演员、喜剧演员、舞蹈家和音乐家来自许多国家。他们在剧院、公园，甚至小商店里表演。游客可以从数千场演出中选择。

The festival is famous for being &#39;open&#39;. There is no committee. Anyone with a ticket can perform. This means new artists have a chance to shine beside famous names. Because of this, the Fringe is very exciting and surprising. You might see an amazing show by someone you have never heard of before.

这个艺术节以‘开放’而闻名。没有评审委员会。只要有票，任何人都可以表演。这意味着新艺术家有机会与名人并肩闪耀。正因为如此，边缘艺术节非常激动人心且充满惊喜。你可能会看到一位从未听说过的人演出的精彩节目。

Besides shows, the atmosphere is full of color and sound. People wear bright costumes and hand out flyers. The city&#39;s old streets become busy with crowds and laughter. If you plan to visit, book early. Hotels fill quickly, and tickets for popular shows disappear fast. But even watching street performers is free and fun.

除了演出，这里的氛围也充满了色彩和声音。人们穿着鲜艳的服装，分发传单。城市的老街变得人来人往，充满笑声。如果你计划去，请提早预订。酒店很快住满，热门演出的票也会迅速卖光。不过，即使只看街头表演，也是免费又有趣的。
