AI and Creative Work: Who Gets the Credit?

Date: 2026-11-24 | Level: B1 (target) | Topic: artificial intelligence and creative work — how AI tools change the idea of authorship and credit

阅读时先抓每段的主旨句，再回看细节；注意作者如何用日常例子解释“署名权”这个抽象概念。

When you use an AI tool to help you write a story or draw a picture, who is the author? This question is becoming important. Many artists and writers now use AI every day. The AI can suggest words, fix grammar, or make a quick sketch. But the ideas and choices still come from the person. For example, a student might ask AI to help plan a short essay. The AI gives a list of points. The student then decides which points to keep and how to explain them. In this case, the human is still the main creator. The AI is more like a helpful assistant than a real author.

当你用AI工具帮你写故事或画画时，作者是谁？这个问题正变得重要。许多艺术家和作家现在每天都用AI。AI可以建议用词、修改语法，或快速画个草图。但想法和选择仍然来自人。例如，一个学生可能请AI帮忙规划一篇短文。AI给出一个要点清单。然后学生决定保留哪些要点、如何解释它们。在这种情况下，人仍然是主要创作者。AI更像一个有用的助手，而不是真正的作者。

However, the line can get blurry. Imagine a designer who asks AI to create ten logo ideas. The AI produces them in seconds. The designer picks one, changes the colors, and uses it for a client. Did the designer "make" the logo? They made important choices, but the first image came from the machine. This is a real trade-off. Using AI saves time and lets people try more ideas. But it can also make it harder to say who did the real work. Some companies now ask workers to write down which parts came from AI. This simple step helps everyone understand the process and give credit fairly.

然而，界限可能变得模糊。想象一位设计师请AI生成十个标志创意。AI几秒钟就做出来了。设计师选了一个，改了颜色，然后给客户使用。这位设计师“制作”了这个标志吗？他们做了重要选择，但最初的图像来自机器。这是一个真实的取舍。使用AI节省时间，让人能尝试更多想法。但它也可能让人更难说清谁做了真正的工作。一些公司现在要求员工写下哪些部分来自AI。这个简单的步骤帮助每个人理解过程，并公平地给予认可。

So what should we do? A good rule is to be honest and clear. If AI helped you, say so. If you did most of the work yourself, you can feel proud of it. The goal is not to ban AI tools. They can be useful and fun. The goal is to keep trust between people. When a reader knows how a piece of work was made, they can respect the person behind it. And when artists are honest, they also protect their own names. In the end, credit is about fairness, not about technology.

那么我们该怎么做？一个好规则是诚实和清楚。如果AI帮了你，就说出来。如果大部分工作是你自己做的，你可以为此感到自豪。目标不是禁止AI工具。它们可以有用又有趣。目标是保持人与人之间的信任。当读者知道一件作品是如何制作的，他们就能尊重背后的人。而当艺术家诚实时，他们也保护了自己的名声。归根结底，认可关乎公平，而不是技术。

Grammar

语法小课堂

一般现在时（描述习惯与普遍事实）

文中原句：Many artists and writers now use AI every day.

结构：主语 + 动词原形/动词-s + 其他

说明：一般现在时用来描述经常发生的动作或普遍情况。句中的 now 表示现在的习惯，动词用原形 use。

仿写：Many students use online dictionaries every day.

许多学生每天使用在线词典。

情态动词 can 表示能力或可能性

文中原句：The AI can suggest words, fix grammar, or make a quick sketch.

结构：主语 + can + 动词原形 + 其他

说明：can 后面直接跟动词原形，表示“能够”做某事。这里列举了AI能做的几个动作，用 or 连接。

仿写：This app can translate simple sentences.

这个应用能翻译简单的句子。

动名词作主语

文中原句：Using AI saves time and lets people try more ideas.

结构：动词-ing + 谓语动词（单数）+ 其他

说明：动名词（动词加-ing）可以像名词一样作句子主语，谓语动词通常用单数形式。这里 Using AI 是主语，saves 是谓语。

仿写：Reading before bed helps you relax.

睡前阅读帮助你放松。
