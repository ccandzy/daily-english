Pet Therapy: How Animals Help Us Feel Better

Date: 2026-08-01 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Pets and Animals

本课学习宠物疗法的好处与相关词汇，并练习描述动物如何帮助人们缓解压力。

Many people today live with a lot of stress from work and busy city life. Scientists have discovered that spending time with animals can reduce stress and improve mood. This simple idea, known as pet therapy, is becoming popular in hospitals, schools, and even offices. Some companies bring dogs to work so employees can feel more relaxed.

今天许多人都因工作和繁忙的城市生活而承受很大压力。科学家发现，花时间与动物相处可以减轻压力并改善情绪。这个简单的想法被称为“宠物疗法”，正在医院、学校和办公室变得流行。一些公司带狗去上班，让员工感到更放松。

Pet therapy is not only about dogs and cats. Some people enjoy watching fish in an aquarium, while others like to hold a small rabbit. The key is the feeling of connection with another living being. When you touch or talk to an animal, your body produces less of the stress hormone cortisol. This helps you feel calm and safe.

宠物疗法不仅仅涉及狗和猫。有些人喜欢看水族箱里的鱼，而另一些人则喜欢抱一只小兔子。关键在于与另一个生命产生联系的感觉。当你触摸或对动物说话时，你的身体会产生较少的压力激素皮质醇。这帮助你感到平静和安全。

However, experts say that pet therapy is not a replacement for medical treatment. It works best as a helpful addition to normal care. Also, animals need proper training and care. If you feel stressed, you might consider visiting a local animal shelter. You can simply spend time with the animals there and enjoy their friendly presence.

然而，专家表示，宠物疗法不能替代药物治疗。它作为正常护理的辅助手段效果最好。此外，动物需要适当的训练和照顾。如果你感到有压力，可以考虑去当地的动物收容所。你可以花时间与那里的动物相处，享受它们友好的陪伴。
