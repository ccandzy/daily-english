The Great Exhibition of 1851

Date: 2026-08-16 | Level: Beginner | Topic: A glass palace and the first world fair

这篇文章介绍1851年伦敦万国工业博览会，以及“水晶宫”的故事。阅读时注意过去时态和描述性词语，试着理解技术如何改变历史。

In 1851, London hosted the Great Exhibition. It was the first international exhibition of industry and art. People from many countries brought their best products to show the world. The exhibition was held in a huge building made of glass and iron, called the Crystal Palace. The exhibition lasted nearly six months. The building was so large that it covered more than 74,000 square meters.

1851年，伦敦举办了万国博览会。这是第一次国际性的工业和艺术展览。许多国家的人们带来了他们最好的产品来展示给世界。展览在一个由玻璃和铁制成的巨大建筑中举行，叫做“水晶宫”。展览持续了近六个月。这座建筑非常大，占地超过七万四千平方米。

Inside the Crystal Palace, visitors could see amazing new machines, colorful fabrics, and valuable treasures. In just five months, more than six million people came to visit. For many visitors, it was their first chance to see things from distant lands. The exhibition made people feel excited about the future and about new inventions.

在水晶宫里面，参观者可以看到惊人的新机器、彩色织物和珍贵的宝物。仅仅五个月内，就有超过六百万人前来参观。对许多参观者来说，这是他们第一次有机会看到来自遥远地方的东西。展览使人们对未来和新发明感到兴奋。

The Great Exhibition showed how industry and technology were changing the world. It also encouraged trade and friendship between countries. After the fair ended, the Crystal Palace was moved to south London. It became a popular attraction for many years, but sadly burned down in 1936. However, the idea of world expos continued around the world. This event influenced many later fairs and reminded people to share new ideas.

万国博览会展示了工业和科技如何改变世界。它也促进了国家之间的贸易和友谊。展会结束后，水晶宫被搬到了伦敦南部。多年来它一直是一个受欢迎的景点，但遗憾的是在1936年被烧毁。然而，世界博览会的理念在全世界得以延续。这一事件影响了后来的许多博览会，并提醒人们分享新思想。
