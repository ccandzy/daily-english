Water Vapor Found on a Distant Planet

Date: 2026-08-07 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Science discovery: water vapor in an exoplanet's atmosphere

阅读技巧：先尝试理解每段英文，再对照中文翻译。注意新词和短语在上下文中的用法。

Scientists recently used a powerful space telescope to study a small, rocky planet far beyond our solar system. For the first time, they found clear signs of water vapor in its atmosphere. Water is very important for life as we know it, so this discovery excites astronomers. However, they warn that having water does not mean the planet is livable.

科学家最近使用一台强大的太空望远镜，研究太阳系外一颗小而多岩石的行星。他们第一次在其大气中发现了明确的水蒸气迹象。水对我们所知的生命非常重要，所以这一发现令天文学家兴奋。然而，他们提醒说，有水并不意味着这颗行星适合生命存在。

The planet travels around a cool, dim star. It sits in what scientists call the habitable zone, where temperatures might allow liquid water. Yet the planet is so far away that we cannot see its surface directly. Instead, scientists study the light that passes through its atmosphere.

这颗行星围绕一颗寒冷、暗淡的恒星运行。它位于科学家所说的宜居带，那里的温度或许允许液态水存在。然而，这颗行星太遥远，我们无法直接看到其表面。相反，科学家研究穿过其大气层的光线。

This finding is a big step in the search for life beyond Earth. Future telescopes will look more closely at similar planets. Scientists hope to find other gases, such as oxygen and methane, that may be produced by living things. Although we have not found alien life yet, every new discovery brings us closer to answering a timeless question: Are we alone in the universe?

这一发现是寻找地球外生命的重要一步。未来的望远镜将更仔细地观测类似的行星。科学家希望找到其他气体，例如氧气和甲烷，这些气体可能由生物产生。虽然我们还没有找到外星生命，但每一个新发现都让我们更接近回答一个永恒的问题：我们在宇宙中是孤独的吗？
