Walk It Off: Small Steps for Better Health

Date: 2026-08-02 | Level: Beginner | Topic: The benefits of a short walk after meals

饭后短距离散步有助于消化和血糖控制。放慢速度，轻松进行。

Many people sit down right after a big meal and feel sleepy. However, a short walk can be a simple and powerful health habit. Instead of lying down, try walking for about 10 minutes. This gentle activity helps your body digest food and keeps your energy level steady. It is easy, free, and good for everyone.

很多人在大餐后马上坐下并感到困倦。然而，短暂散步可能是一种简单而强大的健康习惯。与其躺下，不如试着走大约10分钟。这种温和的活动帮助身体消化食物，并保持能量水平稳定。它简单、免费，而且对每个人都有好处。

Walking after meals also helps control blood sugar. When you move, your muscles use some of the sugar from your food. This can reduce the sudden rise in blood sugar that sometimes happens after eating. Over time, this habit may lower the risk of developing serious health problems.

饭后散步也有助于控制血糖。当你活动时，肌肉会消耗食物中的部分糖分。这可以减少进食后有时出现的血糖突然升高。随着时间的推移，这一习惯可能会降低患严重健康问题的风险。

You do not need to walk fast. A slow, relaxed walk is enough. If you work at a desk, try to include this small routine after lunch or dinner. You can also walk with a friend or listen to music. The key is to make it a daily habit and enjoy the process.

你不需要走很快。缓慢、放松的散步就足够了。如果你在办公桌前工作，试着在午餐或晚餐后加入这个小小的日常安排。你也可以和朋友一起散步，或者听音乐。关键是让它成为日常习惯，并享受这个过程。
