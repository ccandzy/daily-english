The Story of the Sandwich

Date: 2026-08-09 | Level: Beginner | Topic: The historical story of how the sandwich got its name.

学习建议：先听或跟读英文段落，再对照中文理解。注意短语和单词的用法，然后尝试用英文复述这个故事。

The sandwich is one of the world&#39;s favorite foods. You can find it in almost every country. But do you know how it got its name? The story begins in England in the 1700s. A rich man named John Montagu was the fourth Earl of Sandwich. He loved playing card games for hours. He did not want to stop playing to eat a meal.

三明治是世界上最受欢迎的食物之一。你几乎在每个国家都能找到它。但你知道它是怎么得名的吗？故事始于18世纪的英格兰。一个叫约翰·蒙塔古的富人是第四代桑威奇伯爵。他喜欢连续几小时打牌。他不想停下来吃饭。

One night, he asked his servant to bring him some meat between two pieces of bread. This way, he could eat with one hand and play cards with the other. His friends saw this and thought it was a great idea. They began to order &#39;the same as Sandwich!&#39; Soon, the habit spread to other people.

一天晚上，他让仆人拿些肉夹在两片面包中间。这样，他就可以一只手吃东西，另一只手玩牌。他的朋友们看到后，认为这是个好主意。他们开始点“和桑威奇一样的”食物。很快，这个习惯传给了其他人。

Today, we eat sandwiches with many kinds of fillings, such as egg, cheese, chicken, and vegetables. The Earl probably never thought that his simple idea would become so popular. The next time you eat a sandwich, remember the man who loved cards more than meals.

如今，我们吃三明治时会有很多种馅料，比如鸡蛋、奶酪、鸡肉和蔬菜。伯爵大概从未想过，他简单的想法会变得如此受欢迎。下次你吃三明治时，请记住那个爱牌胜过爱饭的人。
