The Rosetta Stone: The Key to Ancient Egypt

Date: 2026-09-01 | Level: Beginner | Topic: A famous historical story about how an old stone helped people understand ancient Egyptian writing.

学习要点：通过罗塞塔石碑的故事，学习一般过去时和描述历史事件的常用词汇。

In 1799, a French soldier found a strange black stone near the town of Rosetta in Egypt. The stone had writing on it in three different scripts. It was an old object, but no one knew how to read the words on it. This stone later became famous all over the world.

1799年，一名法国士兵在埃及罗塞塔镇附近发现了一块奇怪的黑石。石头上用三种不同的文字刻着内容。这是一件古老的物品，但当时没有人知道如何解读上面的文字。这块石头后来闻名于世。

The Rosetta Stone was the key to an old mystery. The same message was written in Greek, Egyptian hieroglyphs, and a simpler Egyptian script. Scholars could read Greek, so they compared the words. After many years of work, they learned how to read hieroglyphs. This opened a door to ancient Egypt.

罗塞塔石碑是解开古老谜团的关键。同样的信息用希腊文、埃及象形文字和一种更简单的埃及文字书写。学者们能读懂希腊文，于是他们比较这些文字。经过多年的努力，他们学会了如何解读象形文字。这为古埃及打开了一扇门。

Today, the Rosetta Stone is in the British Museum in London. Millions of people visit it every year. It reminds us that language can connect us to the past. The stone is not very beautiful, but its story is powerful. It shows how curious people can solve great puzzles.

如今，罗塞塔石碑收藏在伦敦的大英博物馆。每年有数百万人参观它。它提醒我们，语言可以连接我们与过去。这块石头并不十分漂亮，但它的故事很有力量。它展示了好奇的人们如何解开伟大的谜题。

Based on a well-known historical event. The Rosetta Stone is a real artifact in the British Museum.
