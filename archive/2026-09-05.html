Holi: The Festival of Colors

Date: 2026-09-05 | Level: Beginner | Topic: 文化节日 — 印度的洒红节（Holi）

Holi is a famous spring festival in India. People call it the Festival of Colors. Every year, usually in March, families and friends join together to celebrate the end of winter and the coming of warm days. On this day, the streets are full of joy and excited voices.

荷丽节（洒红节）是印度一个著名的春季节日。人们称它为“色彩节”。每年通常在三月，家人和朋友们聚在一起，庆祝冬天的结束和温暖日子的到来。在这一天，街道上充满了欢乐和兴奋的欢呼声。

The best part of Holi is the color play. People throw colorful powder into the air and onto each other. You may see somebody with yellow hands, a blue face, and a pink shirt. Groups of people also walk or ride through the streets with music, and children love shooting water guns. Everyone looks like a moving rainbow.

荷丽节最好玩的部分就是玩色彩。人们把彩色粉末抛向空中，也撒在彼此身上。你可能会看到有人手是黄色的，脸是蓝色的，而衬衫却是粉红色的。成群的人还伴随着音乐在街上行走或骑车，孩子们喜欢玩水枪。每个人看起来都像一道移动的彩虹。

Behind the fun, Holi carries an old story about the victory of good over evil. It is also a season of new beginnings. People say sorry for their mistakes, and they try to “bury the hatchet,” which means making peace with old enemies. Everyone wears simple clothes, forgets past differences, and enjoys a big family dinner.

在欢乐的背后，荷丽节承载着一个“善良战胜邪恶”的古老故事。它也是一个重新开始的季节。人们为自己的错误道歉，并努力“放下斧头”（和解），意思是与旧敌讲和。大家都穿着简单的衣服，忘记过去的分歧，享受一顿丰盛的家庭晚餐。
