Autumn's Quiet Arrival

Date: 2026-08-23 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Weather and seasons in daily life

本课帮助你学习描述季节和天气的词汇，注意短语搭配。

In many places, autumn arrives slowly. The air becomes cooler and the days grow shorter. People begin to wear light jackets and drink warm tea. Leaves change from green to gold and red. This seasonal shift affects our mood and energy. We may feel more calm and want to spend time indoors.

在许多地方，秋天慢慢到来。空气变凉，白天变短。人们开始穿薄外套，喝热茶。树叶从绿色变成金色和红色。这种季节变化影响我们的情绪和精力。我们可能会感到更平静，想要待在室内。

Weather forecasts help us plan our days. When rain is coming, people carry umbrellas or choose indoor activities. In autumn, storms are common in some regions. Farmers watch the sky carefully because rain helps or hurts their crops. Scientists also study seasonal patterns to understand climate change. Simple observations, like noticing the wind direction, can connect us to nature.

天气预报帮助我们计划每天的生活。下雨时，人们带伞或选择室内活动。秋天，某些地区常会有暴风雨。农民仔细观察天空，因为雨水对庄稼有益也有害。科学家也研究季节规律，以了解气候变化。简单的观察，比如注意风向，能让我们与自然产生联系。

Autumn invites us to slow down. We can enjoy the crisp air and the beauty of falling leaves. It is a good time to learn new words about weather, such as &#39;breeze&#39; and &#39;frost.&#39; Observing the seasons helps us appreciate nature&#39;s rhythm. Tomorrow, the weather may change again, but every season has its own charm.

秋天邀请我们放慢脚步。我们可以享受清新的空气和落叶的美丽。这是学习天气相关新词的好时机，比如“微风”和“霜”。观察季节帮助我们欣赏自然的节奏。明天天气可能又会变化，但每个季节都有它独特的魅力。
