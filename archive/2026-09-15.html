The Bicycle That Changed History

Date: 2026-09-15 | Level: Beginner | Topic: A simple history story — the invention of the bicycle

读的时候先抓住时间词（Long ago, Later, Today），它们像路标，帮你看懂故事顺序。

Long ago, people walked or rode horses to travel. In 1817, a German man named Karl von Drais built a simple machine with two wheels. It had no pedals. Riders pushed the ground with their feet to move forward. People laughed at it, but the idea was very important for the future.

很久以前，人们出行靠走路或骑马。1817年，一位名叫卡尔·冯·德莱斯的德国人造出了一台有两个轮子的简单机器。它没有踏板。骑的人用脚蹬地来向前移动。人们嘲笑它，但这个想法对未来非常重要。

Later, inventors added pedals and rubber tires. Riding became faster and more comfortable. In the 1880s, a safer design appeared: two wheels of the same size, with a chain. Suddenly, many people could travel farther than before. For the first time, ordinary workers could visit villages near their towns.

后来，发明家加上了踏板和橡胶轮胎。骑车变得更快、更舒适。19世纪80年代，出现了更安全的设计：两个同样大小的轮子，还有一条链条。突然间，许多人能比以前走得更远。普通工人第一次可以去附近的村庄看看。

Bicycles also changed women&#39;s lives. They could go out alone, without a horse or a driver. Some doctors said riding was good exercise; others worried it was dangerous. Today, millions of people ride bikes to school, to work, or just for fun. It is a simple machine, but it quietly moved history.

自行车也改变了女性的生活。她们可以独自外出，不需要马，也不需要车夫。有些医生说骑车是很好的锻炼；另一些人则担心它很危险。今天，数百万人骑车上学、上班，或者只是为了好玩。它是一台简单的机器，却悄悄推动了历史。
