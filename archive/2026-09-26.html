Cooking Once, Eating Well All Week

Date: 2026-09-26 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Meal prep — cooking ahead for the week

先不看中文，读一遍英文并猜生词的意思，再对照翻译检查。特别注意 meal prep 这类日常生活里的新说法。

Many busy people now spend part of Sunday in the kitchen. They cook rice, vegetables, and chicken for the whole week, then pack everything into small boxes and put them in the fridge. This habit has a simple name: meal prep. It is becoming popular with students and office workers.

现在很多忙碌的人会把周日的部分时间花在厨房里。他们一次性做好一周的米饭、蔬菜和鸡肉，然后把食物装进小盒子里放进冰箱。这个习惯有个简单的名字：备餐。它在学生和上班族中越来越流行。

The idea is easy to understand. You cook once, and later you only heat and eat. On a busy weekday evening, this saves time and money, and you buy fewer takeaways. Some people also say they feel healthier, because they know exactly what is in their food.

这个想法很容易理解。你只做一次饭，之后只需加热就能吃。在忙碌的工作日晚上，这样省时又省钱，买的外卖也少了。有些人还说感觉更健康，因为他们清楚食物里到底有什么。

There is one problem, though. Eating the same dish five times can feel boring. Clever cooks solve this by changing the sauce, adding fresh herbs, or freezing half of the food for later. In short, a little planning can make daily cooking much easier.

不过，有一个问题。同一道菜吃五次会让人觉得腻。聪明的做法是换酱料、加新鲜香草，或者把一半食物冷冻留到以后吃。总之，稍微做点计划，日常做饭就会轻松很多。
