Staying Safe Online Without Losing Your Freedom

Date: 2026-10-16 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Online safety and privacy

学习本课时，注意“隐私设置、密码、陌生人”等常见网络词汇，并练习用完整句子表达建议。

Every day, we share small pieces of our lives online. We post photos, send messages, and click links without much thought. That is normal. However, not everyone on the internet is friendly. Some strangers may try to trick you with fake offers, so it is wise to pause before you share personal details.

每天，我们都在网上分享生活的小片段。我们发照片、发消息、点链接，往往没多想。这很正常。不过，网上的每个人并不都友善。有些陌生人可能用假优惠骗你，所以分享个人信息前先停一停是明智的。

One simple habit helps a lot: check your privacy settings. Decide who can see your posts and who cannot. Use a strong password, and never use the same one everywhere. If an app asks for too much information, you can say no. You are allowed to keep some things private.

一个简单的习惯很有帮助：检查你的隐私设置。决定谁能看到你的帖子，谁不能。使用强密码，不要到处都用同一个。如果某个应用要太多信息，你可以拒绝。你有权保留一些私事。

Remember, safety does not mean fear. It simply means thinking first. If a message feels strange or too good to be true, do not reply. Talk to a trusted friend or adult. Small careful choices protect your time, your money, and your peace of mind. They also protect the people who trust you.

记住，安全并不等于恐惧。它只是意味着先思考。如果一条消息感觉奇怪或好得不像真的，不要回复。和信任的朋友或成年人聊聊。小小的谨慎选择会保护你的时间、金钱和内心的安宁，也会保护信任你的人。
