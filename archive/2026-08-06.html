Visible Mending: A Creative Hobby

Date: 2026-08-06 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Hobbies and crafts: the trend of repairing and creating by hand

先读英文段落，再对照中文翻译，注意手工主题的常用表达。

These days, many people are rediscovering the joy of making things by hand. From knitting to pottery, creative hobbies help people relax and stay away from screens. Crafting is no longer only for children or grandparents. It has become a popular way for young adults to spend free time and express themselves.

如今，很多人重新发现亲手制作东西的乐趣。从编织到陶艺，创意爱好帮助人们放松身心、远离屏幕。手工不再只是孩子或祖父母的活动。它已成为年轻人打发空闲时间、表达自我的流行方式。

In recent years, &#39;visible mending&#39; has caught the attention of many DIY lovers. Instead of hiding a tear in an old shirt, people repair it with colorful thread and make it look special. This practice is both economical and eco-friendly. It also gives old clothes a second life and makes every piece unique.

近年来，“肉眼可见的修补”引起了许多手工爱好者的注意。与其把旧衬衫上的破洞藏起来，人们会用彩色的线把它补好，让它看起来很有特色。这种做法既省钱又环保。它还能让旧衣服获得第二次生命，让每件衣服都独一无二。

If you want to start a craft hobby, begin with a simple kit. For example, you can try weaving a small bag or painting a set of cups. Online videos can teach you the basics step by step. The key is to enjoy the process, not to be perfect. Soon you may find that making something with your own hands is deeply satisfying.

如果你想开始一项手工爱好，可以从一个简单的工具包开始。例如，你可以尝试编织一个小包或绘制一套杯子。在线视频可以一步一步教你基础知识。关键在于享受过程，而不是追求完美。很快你可能会发现，用自己的双手制作东西会带来深深的满足感。
