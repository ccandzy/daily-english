A Small Battery with a Big Idea

Date: 2026-10-20 | Level: Beginner | Topic: science discoveries

阅读时注意 because、so、but 等连接词，它们帮助你看清句子之间的逻辑关系。

Scientists are testing a new battery made from salt and water. It is cheaper and safer than many common batteries. The idea is not new, but the team has made it work better. They say this battery can store energy from the sun and wind. In the future, it may help homes use clean power.

科学家正在测试一种由盐和水制成的新电池。它比许多常见电池更便宜、更安全。这个想法并不新，但团队让它运行得更好。他们说这种电池可以储存来自太阳和风能。未来，它可能帮助家庭使用清洁能源。

The battery is not very strong yet. It cannot power a car for a long time. However, it is good for storing electricity for a few hours. That is useful because solar power only works during the day. At night, the battery can give power to lights and small machines. This helps people save money and energy.

这种电池还不够强。它不能长时间为汽车供电。然而，它适合储存几个小时的电。这很有用，因为太阳能只在白天工作。晚上，电池可以为灯和小型机器供电。这帮助人们节省金钱和能源。

The scientists are working to make the battery stronger and longer-lasting. They also want to make it smaller. If they succeed, it could be used in many places, from small villages to big cities. This discovery shows that simple materials can lead to important changes.

科学家正努力让电池更强、更耐用。他们还想让它更小。如果他们成功，它可以在许多地方使用，从小村庄到大城市。这一发现表明，简单的材料可以带来重要的变化。
