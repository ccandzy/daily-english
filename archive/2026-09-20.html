Small Habits, Better English

Date: 2026-09-20 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Language learning tips

每天短时间学习，并大胆开口，进步会更稳。

Learning a new language can feel slow, but small daily habits help a lot. Instead of studying for three hours once a week, try fifteen focused minutes every day. Review old words before adding new ones. This simple routine strengthens your memory and keeps you moving forward.

学一门新语言可能感觉缓慢，但每天的小习惯很有帮助。不要一周一次学三小时，试着每天专注十五分钟。先复习旧词，再加新词。这个简单习惯能增强记忆，让你不断前进。

Speaking is often the hardest part. You may worry about mistakes, but mistakes are normal. When you speak, people usually care about your meaning, not perfect grammar. Practice short sentences aloud. Slow, clear speech is better than fast, unclear speech. You can talk to yourself or chat with a patient friend.

口语通常是最难的部分。你可能担心犯错，但犯错很正常。当你说话时，人们通常在意你的意思，而不是完美的语法。大声练习短句。慢而清楚比快而不清更好。你可以自言自语，或和有耐心的朋友聊天。

Listening also matters. Choose easy podcasts or videos that you enjoy. If you understand most of it, the level is right for you. Do not stop at every unknown word. Try to catch the main idea first. After listening, write down two or three new expressions. Over time, these tiny steps will build real confidence.

听力也很重要。选择你喜欢且简单的播客或视频。如果你能听懂大部分，这个难度就适合你。不要每个生词都停下来。先试着抓住大意。听完后，写下两三个新表达。久而久之，这些小步骤会建立真正的信心。
