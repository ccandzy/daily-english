Slow Travel: A Better Way to See the World

Date: 2026-09-14 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Travel and tourism — slow travel

先读一遍英文，抓住大意，再对照中文检查细节。注意 "instead of" 后面接名词或动名词。

Many travelers today choose slow travel. Instead of visiting five cities in one week, they stay in one place for a longer time. They walk in local markets, eat simple food, and talk with people who live there. This way, they learn more about real daily life, and they feel less tired.

如今许多旅行者选择"慢旅行"。他们不再一周内游览五座城市，而是在一个地方待得更久。他们在当地市场散步，吃简单的食物，和住在那里的人聊天。这样，他们能更了解真实的日常生活，也不会那么累。

Slow travel is also better for the planet. Trains and buses create less pollution than short flights. You can also save money, because you do not pay for many tickets. Some people say the journey itself becomes the best part of the holiday.

慢旅行对地球也更好。火车和巴士比短途航班造成的污染更少。你还能省钱，因为不用买很多张票。有人说，旅程本身就变成了假期中最棒的部分。

If you want to try it, start small. Choose a town near your city and stay for three nights. Leave your phone in your bag sometimes, and just look around you. You may feel calm and relaxed. Best of all, you will remember this short trip for a long time.

如果你想尝试，就从小事开始。选一个离你所在城市不远的小镇，住上三晚。有时把手机放在包里，只是看看周围。你可能会感到平静又放松。最棒的是，这段短短的旅程你会记很久。
