Small Habits, Big Change: A Simple Daily Routine

Date: 2026-10-14 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Daily Routines

读的时候先找出每段的“动作词”，例如 wake up、put away，这样更容易记住日常习惯的表达。

Many people believe that a perfect morning routine will change their whole life. In fact, you do not need to wake up at five o&#39;clock or run five kilometres before breakfast. A useful routine is simply a small set of actions you repeat every day, so your brain does not have to decide again and again.

很多人相信，完美的早晨习惯会改变整个人生。事实上，你不需要五点起床，也不用早餐前跑五公里。有用的习惯只是一小套你每天重复的动作，这样你的大脑就不用一次又一次地做决定。

Start with something easy. For example, put your phone away for the first twenty minutes after you get up. Drink a glass of water, open the window, and write down the two most important tasks for the day. These habits take only a few minutes, but they help you feel calm and ready.

从简单的事开始。例如，起床后的前二十分钟把手机放远一点。喝一杯水，打开窗户，写下当天最重要的两项任务。这些习惯只花几分钟，却能让你感到平静和准备好。

Of course, some days are busy and your plan may fail. That is fine. Experts say it is better to keep the routine simple than to make it perfect. If you miss one morning, just begin again the next day. Slowly, these small steps become part of your normal life.

当然，有些日子很忙，你的计划可能会失败。没关系。专家说，让习惯保持简单，比让它变得完美更好。如果错过了一个早上，第二天重新开始就好。慢慢地，这些小步骤会成为你正常生活的一部分。
