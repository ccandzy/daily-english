Move a Little, Feel Better

Date: 2026-10-09 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Health and Exercise

本课练习"日常小动作"相关表达。注意 in fact、instead of 这类连接短语，它们能让句子更自然、更像母语者说话。

Many people believe that exercise must be long and hard to be useful. In fact, doctors say that short moments of movement during the day can help a lot. A ten-minute walk after lunch, for example, can make you feel more awake and less tired in the afternoon. It also helps you sleep better at night.

很多人认为运动必须又长又累才有用。事实上，医生说白天短时间的活动也能帮上大忙。比如午饭后走十分钟，就能让你下午更清醒、不那么累。它还能帮你晚上睡得更好。

You do not need a gym or expensive clothes. Take the stairs instead of the lift. Stand up and stretch your arms while you are waiting for the kettle to boil. Even two minutes of movement count. These small habits are easy to keep, and they quietly protect your heart.

你不需要健身房，也不需要昂贵的衣服。走楼梯而不坐电梯。等水壶烧开时站起来伸展手臂。哪怕两分钟的活动也算数。这些小习惯容易坚持，还能默默保护你的心脏。

The best exercise is the one you actually do. Choose an activity you enjoy, start slowly, and add a few minutes each week. If you feel sharp pain or have a health problem, talk to a doctor first. Your body will thank you later, in many ways.

最好的运动就是你真的去做的运动。选一个你喜欢的活动，慢慢开始，每周多加几分钟。如果你感到剧痛，或者有健康问题，先去看医生。以后你的身体会用很多方式感谢你。
