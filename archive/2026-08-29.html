The Wonderful World of Homemade Soup

Date: 2026-08-29 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Food and Cooking

学习如何描述简单的烹饪过程，并谈论自制食物。

Making a pot of homemade soup is easier than you think. You only need a few fresh vegetables, some broth, and a little time. First, chop the onions, carrots, and potatoes into small pieces. Then, heat some oil in a large pot and cook the vegetables until they are soft. This simple step brings out their natural sweetness.

做一锅家常汤比你想象的要容易。你只需要一些新鲜蔬菜、一点高汤和一点时间。首先，把洋葱、胡萝卜和土豆切成小块。然后，在大锅里加热一些油，把蔬菜炒到变软。这个简单的步骤能带出它们的天然甜味。

Next, add the broth and bring it to a boil. After that, lower the heat and let the soup simmer for about twenty minutes. The longer it cooks, the richer the flavor becomes. You can also add herbs like thyme or a pinch of black pepper to make it more interesting. Many people like to blend the soup to make it smooth, but you can keep it chunky if you prefer.

接下来，加入高汤并煮沸。之后，调小火，让汤炖煮大约二十分钟。煮得越久，味道越浓郁。你还可以加入百里香之类的香草或一小撮黑胡椒，让它更有风味。很多人喜欢把汤打成糊状使其顺滑，但如果你喜欢，也可以保留块状。

Homemade soup is not only tasty but also healthy. It is a great way to use up vegetables that are about to go bad in your fridge. When you cook from scratch, you control the amount of salt and oil. This makes it a better choice than canned soup. Try making a pot this weekend. You will be surprised by how satisfying it is.

家常汤不仅美味，而且健康。这是用完冰箱里即将变质的蔬菜的好办法。当你从头开始烹饪时，你可以控制盐和油的用量。这使它比罐装汤更健康。这个周末试着做一锅吧。你会惊讶于它是多么令人满足。
