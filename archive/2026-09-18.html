New Year Traditions Around the World

Date: 2026-09-18 | Level: Beginner | Topic: Holidays and Celebrations

读的时候先抓每段的"中心意思"，再回头看细节。注意本课里表示"习俗、传统"的词，它们常一起出现。

Every country has its own way to welcome a new year. In China, families gather for a big dinner and stay up late to watch fireworks. In Spain, people eat twelve grapes at midnight — one grape for each lucky month. In Brazil, many people wear white clothes and jump over small waves at the beach.

每个国家都有自己迎接新年的方式。在中国，家人聚在一起吃大餐，并熬夜看烟花。在西班牙，人们在午夜吃十二颗葡萄——每颗代表一个幸运的月份。在巴西，许多人穿白色衣服，在海滩上跳过小海浪。

These traditions look different, but they share a simple idea. People want to say goodbye to the old year and hope for a better one. They also want to spend time with the people they love. Food, music and small gifts often help everyone feel close and happy.

这些传统看起来不同，但它们有一个简单的共同点。人们想告别旧的一年，并期待更好的一年。他们也想和爱的人一起度过时光。食物、音乐和小礼物常常让大家感到亲近和快乐。

If you travel during a holiday, you may see celebrations that feel strange at first. Try to join in and ask questions. Local people are usually glad to explain their customs. You do not need perfect language — a smile and a friendly &quot;Happy New Year&quot; are often enough.

如果你在节日期间旅行，可能会看到一开始觉得很奇怪的庆祝活动。试着加入进去，并提一些问题。当地人通常乐意解释他们的风俗。你不需要完美的语言——一个微笑和一句友好的"新年快乐"往往就够了。
