The First Hot Air Balloon

Date: 2026-10-10 | Level: Beginner | Topic: A simple history story — the first hot air balloon flight

读的时候注意时间顺序（long ago → at first → two months later → today），这样更容易记住故事。

Long ago, people dreamed of flying like birds. In 1783, two French brothers, Joseph and Étienne Montgolfier, found a way. They made a large bag from paper and cloth. When they heated the air inside, the bag rose into the sky. Hot air is lighter than cool air, so it lifted the balloon.

很久以前，人们梦想像鸟一样飞。1783年，法国两兄弟约瑟夫和艾蒂安·蒙戈尔菲耶找到了办法。他们用纸和布做了一个大袋子。当他们把里面的空气加热时，袋子升上了天空。热空气比冷空气轻，所以它把气球托了起来。

At first, the brothers were not sure if flying was safe for people. So they sent three animals up in a basket: a sheep, a duck, and a rooster. The balloon rose high above the crowd and came down in a field. All three animals were fine. This gave people courage to try.

起初，兄弟俩不确定飞行对人是否安全。于是他们把三只动物放在篮子里送上去：一只羊、一只鸭子和一只公鸡。气球高高升过人群，最后落在一片田野里。三只动物都安然无恙。这给了人们尝试的勇气。

Two months later, two brave men flew over Paris in a hot air balloon. Crowds clapped and cheered as it drifted above the rooftops. Soon, ballooning became a popular hobby in Europe. Today, hot air balloons still float quietly in the sky, and the idea behind them has never changed.

两个月后，两名勇敢的男子乘热气球飞越巴黎。气球飘过屋顶时，人群鼓掌欢呼。不久，乘热气球在欧洲成了一种流行的爱好。今天，热气球依然静静地飘在天空，而它背后的原理从未改变。
